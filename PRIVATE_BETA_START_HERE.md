# Private Beta Start Here

Father's Business Bible Study is ready for a small trusted beta focused on Bible reading, study depth, listening, library reading, prayer, journaling, sermon preparation, and presentation basics.

## Main Test Path

1. Open the app on a phone first.
2. Read John 3.
3. Open the Passage Guide.
4. Tap a key word and check Webster/Strong's starter lookup.
5. Open commentary for John 3.
6. Add a note, highlight, and bookmark.
7. Listen to the chapter, then press Stop.
8. Open Library and search for an author or topic.
9. Open one book and test Read and Listen.
10. Create one prayer request.
11. Create one journal entry from a verse.
12. Open Sermons and load or create a John 3 lesson.
13. Open slides/present mode briefly.
14. Export your notes or settings data before heavy testing.
15. Submit feedback from Settings or the feedback link.

## Best Passages To Test

- John 3
- Romans 8
- Amos 1
- Daniel 7
- Revelation 13

These chapters currently have stronger beta coverage for commentary, TSK samples, Strong's starter entries, and background data.

## What To Report

- Anything that looks broken, cramped, confusing, or unfinished.
- Any button that does nothing without explaining why.
- Any listening flow where Stop does not stop playback.
- Any note, highlight, prayer, journal, sermon, playlist, or reading progress that disappears after refresh.
- Any public page that shows internal planning notes instead of polished user-facing text.

## Beta Safety

- Signed-out data is stored on the device/browser.
- Use exports before adding serious personal notes.
- Do not rely on beta sermon slides for live church use without reviewing them carefully.
- Browser/device voices vary. Apple voices may only appear in Safari or specific device/browser combinations.
