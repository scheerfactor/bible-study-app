## Purpose

This packet is for contacting authors, publishers, ministries, pastors, teachers, audiobook projects, and sermon/media rights holders about permission to include selected resources in Father's Business Bible Study.

The first goal is not to request an entire catalog. The first goal is to build trust, learn each rights holder's process, and request narrow permission for one title, one excerpt, one sample chapter, one sermon, or one audio item.

## What The App Is

Father's Business Bible Study is a Scripture-first Bible study app for Christians, Sunday School teachers, pastors, missionaries, evangelists, and serious Bible students.

The app helps users:

- read Scripture
- study Scripture
- use public-domain commentaries and Bible tools
- journal and pray from Scripture
- prepare lessons and sermons
- organize library reading
- prepare sermon slides and preaching notes

## Rights Safety Promise

Use this wording consistently:

Father's Business Bible Study does not publicly publish copyrighted books, commentaries, sermons, audio, video, scans, or excerpts unless permission is documented. Public resources are limited to public-domain or permission-cleared material. Unapproved resources remain hidden from normal users.

## Current Proof Points

- Working private beta app
- KJV-focused Bible reader and study workflow
- More than 1,300 public-domain library resources
- More than 11,000 public commentary entries
- Full chapter-level coverage for several verified public-domain commentary sets
- Admin-only rights tracking and review workflow
- Public/private resource separation
- Audio and video intake stays rights-first

Do not claim the app has complete verse-level commentary segmentation yet. The major verified commentary sets have full chapter coverage, while verse-level indexing is a separate improvement pass.

## First Ask Strategy

Start small.

Best first ask:

1. Permission to list the title, author, description, and official link.
2. Permission to show one approved excerpt or sample chapter.
3. Permission to include one selected resource in private beta.
4. Permission to discuss full digital text later.
5. Permission to discuss audio, TTS, audiobook, or video rights separately.

Avoid asking for broad catalog rights in the first message unless the rights holder already offers a formal licensing process.

## Full Digital Permission Request

Use only after the rights holder responds positively or asks for specific details.

```text

I would like to request permission to include [TITLE] by [AUTHOR] in Father's Business Bible Study.

Requested use:

- display the full text inside the app
- allow search inside the title
- allow bookmarks, reading progress, highlights, and personal notes
- recommend the resource in related Bible study workflows
- show author, publisher, source, and rights metadata

Please let me know:

- whether this is permitted
- whether the permission applies to free beta, free public access, paid access, or all of these
- whether excerpts may be copied into notes or exports
- what attribution is required
- whether audio/TTS rights are separate
- any royalty, reporting, term length, or removal requirements

Nothing will be published until the terms are documented.

Thank you,

Stephen Scheer
hello@fathersbusinessmasteryresources.com
```

## Status Rules

- No reply: keep as `Permission Needed`.
- Verbal interest only: keep as `Contacted` or `Negotiating`.
- Written approval with clear terms: mark `Approved`.
- Unclear terms: mark `Needs Review`.
- Denial: mark `Denied`.
- Personal/private use only: mark `Personal Use Only`.
- Do not import: mark `Do Not Import`.

## Important Cautions

- Owning a physical book does not grant digital distribution rights.
- Public availability online does not always mean public domain.
- Modern reprints may have edited material, introductions, notes, formatting, or cover art under copyright.
- Audio rights are separate from text rights.
- Sermon transcripts, photos, music, and video may each have separate rights.
- No response does not equal permission.
