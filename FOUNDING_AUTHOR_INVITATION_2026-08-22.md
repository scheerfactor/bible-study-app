# Founding Author And Ministry Invitation

Prepared 2026-08-22 for review. This document does not grant rights and does not authorize automatic outreach.

## Public Positioning

Use an affirmative description instead of attacking other churches, publishers, authors, or software:

> Father's Business Bible Study is a KJV-centered, conservative Baptist-friendly study platform built to help Christians read, understand, obey, teach, and preach Scripture. Scripture remains primary. Supporting resources are admitted through source, rights, quality, and doctrinal review, and their author, date, perspective, and cautions remain visible.

This is stronger and more trustworthy than making blanket claims about every resource produced outside the project's doctrinal position. The app can clearly decline unsuitable content while treating people truthfully and respectfully.

## Ninety-Second Explanation

Father's Business Bible Study connects the work that pastors, teachers, and serious Bible students already do: read the KJV, define a word, inspect Strong's and cross-references, consult reviewed commentary, capture application, build a lesson or sermon, and prepare a presentation.

The library is curated instead of treated as an unmarked warehouse. Every resource must have source and rights evidence. Modern copyrighted works remain private or absent until written permission defines what may be listed, quoted, displayed, hosted, narrated, presented, or sold. Historical works may carry perspective and caution labels because inclusion is not blanket endorsement.

The first partnership request is intentionally small. We would like to represent one useful resource accurately, send readers to its official source, and earn trust before discussing broader digital or commercial rights.

## Request Menu

Ask for each item separately so a rights holder can say yes to one use without accidentally granting another:

1. Metadata and official outbound link
2. Approved description or back-cover copy
3. Cover art or named promotional image
4. Exact excerpt or sample length
5. Private beta access
6. Full searchable text
7. Personal notes, highlights, and bookmarks
8. Lesson, sermon, presentation, or handout use
9. Official audio/video link or embed
10. Audio/video storage or streaming
11. Transcript and text-to-speech rights
12. Free, subscription, church, school, affiliate, or resale terms

## Partnership Safeguards

- Start with one named title or resource, never an implied catalog request.
- Verify the official rights holder and current contact route on the day of sending.
- Send only after Stephen approves the exact recipient, subject, and body.
- Record the message immediately in the send log.
- Treat silence as no permission.
- Store the complete reply and translate it into title-level scope before publication.
- Provide a correction, restriction, revocation, and removal path.
