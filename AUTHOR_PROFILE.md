# Author Profile Template

## Purpose

Every significant author or ministry should have a profile before their resources are treated as a major collection in the app. The profile helps users understand the author's historical setting, strengths, perspective, and permission status.

This is a template. Do not treat an author profile as permission to import all works by that author.
