# Radio and Content Partner Outreach Packet

## Purpose

This packet supports narrow, relationship-first outreach to authors, preachers, publishers, churches, and broadcasters. It does not authorize any content use. Every title, recording, image, transcript, excerpt, stream, download, presentation use, paid-access use, affiliate arrangement, and resale right must be recorded separately.

Father's Business Bible Study should offer partners three clear paths:

1. Official discovery links that send users to the partner's website or store.
2. Free radio or study use under a written, title-level permission.
3. Licensed paid access or in-app sales under a separate commercial agreement.

Never place a free ministry program behind a paid gate unless the rights holder expressly permits that use. A general permission to link or embed does not permit rehosting, downloading, editing, transcription, presentation export, paid access, resale, or AI/TTS use.

## Rights Questions for Every Radio Partner

Ask the partner to answer each item separately:

| Use | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Metadata | May the app show the exact program title, speaker, description, series, and official link? |
| Artwork | May the app display the official program, ministry, author, or book image? |
| Official link | May the app open the partner's official listening or purchase page? |
| Official embed | May the app place the partner's official player inside the free radio area? |
| Direct stream | May the app stream an official audio URL without copying the file? |
| Rehosting | May the app store and serve an unaltered copy? |
| Scheduling | May the program appear in an automated station queue or published schedule? |
| Excerpts | May the app play a named excerpt, bumper, preview, or sample? |
| Transcript | May the app display, search, quote, or index a transcript? |
| Presentation | May approved excerpts, quotes, images, or program clips be used in sermon slides? |
| Free access | Is the permission limited to the free public area? |
| Paid access | May the content appear in a subscription or paid collection? |
| Book sales | May the app use official store links, affiliate links, or licensed in-app sales? |
| Reporting | Does the partner require usage, stream, click, or sales reports? |
| Term | Is permission perpetual, revocable, or limited to a stated date? |

## Commercial Terms to Record

Do not accept a commercial arrangement based only on an informal statement. Record:

- exact title, edition, files, artwork, and identifiers
- territories and languages
- free, paid, subscription, institutional, and offline rights
- wholesale price, revenue share, royalty, or affiliate percentage
- refunds, taxes, payment timing, statements, and audit rights
- term, renewal, termination, and content-removal process
- update and replacement-file responsibility
- usage and sales reporting
- customer support responsibility
- whether excerpts, search, highlights, notes, printing, presentation, and accessibility are allowed
- explicit exclusions for AI training, generated narration, summaries, or derivative work unless separately granted

Use qualified legal and tax counsel before signing material catalog, royalty, exclusivity, or marketplace agreements.

## Outreach Rules

- One title or one program per first request.
- Reply in an existing thread when a request is already pending.
- Do not send a second request before the recorded follow-up date.
- No reply means no permission.
- Keep free-radio permission separate from paid access and book sales.
- Preserve the exact reply and its headers as evidence.
- Thank every partner promptly and confirm the scope in plain language.
- Remove content promptly if permission expires or is withdrawn.
