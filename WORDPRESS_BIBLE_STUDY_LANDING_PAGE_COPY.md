- Compare reviewed public-domain commentary beside the passage.

## A Curated Christian Library

The growing library includes verified public-domain books, commentaries, Bible dictionaries, topical helps, preaching resources, biographies, devotional works, hymns, maps, and carefully reviewed audio.

Modern copyrighted books, audio, and video are not copied merely because they are useful. They are listed, quoted, hosted, streamed, sold, searched, or linked to passages only within documented source and permission terms.

## Authors, Publishers, And Ministry Partners

We are seeking trusted authors, publishers, preachers, schools, and ministries who want to help serious Bible students find and use strong resources responsibly.

Possible partnerships include:

- approved resource listings and official purchase links
- samples and excerpts
- full-text books that readers can purchase and unlock
- searchable commentaries linked to relevant Bible passages
- audiobooks, sermons, Bible classes, radio programs, and videos
- individual, family, church, school, seminary, and ministry licensing

Rights holders keep control of their work. Every commercial or digital use requires written terms covering the exact title, edition, format, access, payment, attribution, reporting, corrections, and removal process.
