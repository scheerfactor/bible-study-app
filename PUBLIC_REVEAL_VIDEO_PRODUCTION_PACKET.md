## Purpose

This packet turns `PUBLIC_REVEAL_AND_VIDEO_PLAN.md` into two recordable public videos. Every visible feature must come from the production release candidate. Do not substitute mockups, hide meaningful wait time, or announce copyrighted resources before written permission is recorded.

The public promise is:

> Read. Understand. Obey. Teach.

The founding free public beta has a working target of Thursday, April 15, 2027 at 7:00 PM Eastern. Every date reference must also say `working target` and remain subject to the published launch gates.

## Required Recording Setup

- Use a clean browser profile with no personal notes, email address, admin controls, acquisition queues, or private permission records visible.
- Record a production build at 1920x1080 and a second clean pass at 390x844.
- Turn off browser bookmarks, unrelated tabs, notifications, autofill suggestions, and password prompts.
- Use only reviewed public resources with visible source and rights evidence.
- Use Hosea 4 for the complete study demonstration and John 3 for a familiar short example.
- Show normal load time. A cut may shorten repetition, but it must not imply an instant response when the actual feature is slow.
- Record pointer movement deliberately and leave each result visible long enough to read.
- Export a captioned and an uncaptioned master.

> Help test one complete task: read a KJV passage, study a word, follow a cross-reference, save a conclusion, and prepare something you could teach. Report what was slow, confusing, missing, or broken.

## Recording Approval Checklist

Do not publish either video until every answer is yes.

- Is every feature shown working in the deployed release candidate?
- Is every visible Bible quotation KJV?
- Is every visible third-party resource public domain or covered by written permission?
- Are source, rights, and doctrinal labels readable where relevant?
- Is all private information removed?
- Does the recording avoid admin-only screens?
- Does the video say `working target` when it shows April 15, 2027?
- Does the video avoid claiming that the app replaces Logos, Proclaim, e-Sword, or another product?
- Do caption text and narration make the same claims?
- Have desktop and vertical exports been watched from beginning to end?
- Has one trusted pastor or teacher reviewed the final cut for clarity and doctrinal wording?
