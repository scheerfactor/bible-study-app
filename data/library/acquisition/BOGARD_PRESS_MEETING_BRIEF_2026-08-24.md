# Bogard Press Partnership Meeting Brief

Prepared: 2026-08-24

Status: Nathan Luellen, Editor in Chief of the Baptist Sunday School Committee / Bogard Press, invited
a phone or Teams discussion. No content permission or partnership has been granted.

## Meeting Goal

Secure agreement on one small, reversible pilot for *Baptists and the Bible* and identify the correct
Bogard Press process for title-level digital licensing, publisher review, and a possible future catalog
relationship.

## Thirty-Minute Agenda

1. **Five minutes: vision and audience**
   - KJV-centered Bible reading and study
   - pastors, Sunday School teachers, missionaries, evangelists, families, and serious students
   - passage study, lesson preparation, sermon preparation, library, and church presentation
2. **Five minutes: private demonstration**
   - passage-first study workflow
   - library discovery and official purchase routes
   - rights labels and blocked content states
   - lesson and presentation workflow
3. **Ten minutes: one-title pilot**
   - exact title and edition
   - approved publisher description
   - official purchase link
   - approved cover or promotional image
   - one approved excerpt or sample, if Bogard Press wants one included
   - attribution and publisher-review process
4. **Five minutes: commercial path**
   - official-link referral first
   - later purchaser-only access or account entitlement only under written terms
   - price, publisher share, app share, reporting, refunds, updates, customer support, and withdrawal
5. **Five minutes: counsel and next action**
   - what pastors and teachers need most
   - what Bogard Press wants protected
   - one named decision-maker, one pilot owner, and one written next step

## Questions To Ask

- Does Bogard Press own every proposed right for the exact edition of *Baptists and the Bible*, or are
  any author, image, translation, or third-party approvals also required?
- Which title elements may be displayed before purchase: title, description, cover, sample pages,
  excerpt, table of contents, and topic or Scripture references?
- May an approved purchaser read licensed content inside the app, or should the pilot initially direct
  users only to Bogard Press?
- May approved content be indexed by Bible passage or topic? If so, may search results show snippets?
- May a church purchaser present an approved excerpt or lesson slide? What printing and export limits
  apply?
- Would Bogard Press prefer referral links, wholesale codes, revenue sharing, publisher-managed
  entitlements, or another sales model?
- What reporting, attribution, branding, updates, takedown, and customer-support process is required?
- Which other adult curriculum or commentary title would Bogard Press consider after a successful
  pilot?

## Proposed First Pilot Boundary

Allowed only if Bogard Press confirms each item in writing:

- official title and publisher-approved description;
- official purchase link;
- one approved cover or promotional image;
- one approved excerpt or sample, with exact length and context;
- exact attribution and review language; and
- a defined test period and withdrawal process.

Remain blocked unless separately licensed:

- full text or downloadable files;
- answer keys, teacher aids, student pages, or classroom copies;
- audio, video, narration, text-to-speech, or transcripts;
- Bible-passage indexing, search snippets, AI summaries, or derivative notes;
- presentation export or unrestricted printing;
- paid access, resale, subscription bundling, or account unlocks; and
- any other title or the broader Bogard Press catalog.

## Demonstration Checklist

- Open the live app at https://study.fathersbusinessmasteryresources.com/
- Show one KJV passage and the passage-first study workflow.
- Show the library rights label and official-link-only behavior.
- Show how a publisher-approved resource could be kept separate from public-domain resources.
- Show lesson preparation and presentation without displaying unlicensed Bogard Press content.
- End with the one-title written-rights checklist, not a broad verbal request.

## Desired Written Follow-Up

Ask Nathan to send or approve a short title-level scope identifying:

- exact title and edition;
- rights holder;
- allowed metadata, cover, excerpt, linking, indexing, presentation, and commercial uses;
- attribution;
- territory and term;
- price or revenue share;
- reporting and user entitlement;
- printing and export limits;
- updates and customer support; and
- withdrawal or termination.

Until that written scope arrives, the app must continue to show no Bogard Press title content.
