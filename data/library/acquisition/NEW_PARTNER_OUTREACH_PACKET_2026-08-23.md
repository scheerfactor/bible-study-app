## Why these prospects come first

1. **Bible Visuals International** can address several current gaps at once: KJV-oriented visual Bible lessons,
   hymns, stories, images, PowerPoint, and a documented licensing process.
2. **Majesty Music** is a strong fit for hymn discovery, piano and instrumental material, recordings,
   congregational projection, and church-music teaching.
3. **Positive Action for Christ** is a focused teacher and curriculum prospect for Sunday school,
   Christian school, homeschool, and youth preparation.
4. **Regular Baptist Press** has KJV-labelled curriculum plus commentaries and ministry resources, but its
   mixed-version and third-party catalog requires a narrow title-by-title approach.
5. **Associates for Biblical Research** is a high-value archaeology and image prospect where claims,
   image ownership, and permissions must be reviewed item by item.
6. **Abeka** is a later formal-partnership prospect because its ordinary product terms do not allow the
   copying, indexing, adaptation, commercial use, or app distribution being considered.

## Send controls

- Recheck Inbox, Spam, and Sent before contacting any organization already in an earlier outreach file.
- Send only one new message at a time and record the exact recipient, timestamp, subject, and body.
- Do not attach copyrighted samples from the prospect's own catalog.
- Do not describe a prospect as a partner, approved publisher, or participating ministry before a written reply.
- Record every allowed use separately: metadata, links, covers, excerpts, full text, search/indexing,
  audio, video, presentation, export, commercial access, and resale.
- Review doctrine and resource suitability separately from copyright permission. Permission does not
  equal recommendation, and doctrinal agreement does not equal permission.
