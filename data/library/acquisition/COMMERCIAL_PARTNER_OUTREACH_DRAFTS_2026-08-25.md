## Striving Together Publications

**To:** info@strivingtogether.com
**Subject:** Possible Bible study software partnership for Striving Together resources

Dear Striving Together Publications Team,

My name is Stephen Scheer, and I am building Father's Business Bible Study, a KJV-centered Bible study and ministry-preparation application. The goal is to help pastors, teachers, families, and serious Bible students move quickly from a passage to trustworthy study, sermon or lesson preparation, and presentation.

I would like to ask whether Striving Together would consider a small digital-library pilot with one title or study guide that you select. A licensed resource could be listed in the app for purchase and unlocked only for that purchaser. With your written approval, it could also be searchable and linked to the Bible passages it discusses. Sales reporting, pricing, the publisher and author share, customer support, refunds, territory, term, and takedown procedures would all be agreed in writing before launch.

I am also interested in your counsel: what would make a Bible study application genuinely useful to your authors, churches, and ministry leaders without diminishing the value of the printed resources?

Nothing from your catalog will be copied, hosted, sold, indexed, or presented as approved unless Striving Together grants exact written permission. I would be glad to provide a short private demonstration and begin with one modest pilot rather than a broad request.

Thank you for considering it.

Stephen Scheer
