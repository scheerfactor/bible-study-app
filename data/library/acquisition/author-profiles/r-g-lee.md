## Verified Background

Robert G. Lee (1886-1978) pastored Bellevue Baptist Church in Memphis from 1927 to 1960 and was especially known for preaching *Payday Someday*. A 1978 United States Congressional Record memorial identifies the sermon as first preached in 1919 and notes that it was recorded, filmed, and translated. This establishes historical importance, not present-day reuse rights.

Sources reviewed 2026-08-30:

- United States Congressional Record memorial: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1978-pt24/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1978-pt24-2-1.pdf

## Rights Separation

Do not treat one permission as covering every use. Ask separately about:

- factual metadata and an official purchase link
- approved cover image and description
- a short marked excerpt
- full sermon text or searchable transcript
- original sermon recording and master rights
- streaming, embedding, downloading, and playlist/radio use
- a newly recorded human or AI/TTS narration
- sermon-slide quotations or derivative presentation material
- free access, subscription access, sale, and revenue sharing

An audio or transcript page on SermonIndex, SermonAudio, a podcast, or another archive is discovery evidence only. It does not by itself prove that the uploader can grant app redistribution or commercial rights.

## Contact Plan

1. Use B&H Publishing’s official copyright/permissions form for the 1998 text edition: https://www.bhpublishinggroup.com/contact-us/
2. Ask B&H whether it controls only the published text/edition or can identify the Lee estate or other successor for broader rights.

## Doctrinal Review
