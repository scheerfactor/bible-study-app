## Biography And Ministry Fit

The official Vance Havner site identifies Havner as a North Carolina preacher and writer whose
ministry began in childhood and continued through decades of evangelistic preaching and writing.
CLC Publications maintains an author page and currently publishes Havner titles. His concise
devotional and preaching style makes the catalog relevant to sermon preparation, Christian
living, revival, and devotional study.

Theological and editorial approval still belongs at title level. Historical usefulness does not
establish agreement with every interpretation, nor does an author-level profile replace review
for Gospel clarity, Scripture handling, KJV wording, Baptist doctrine, dispensational and
premillennial teaching, church doctrine, or application.

Official sources:

- https://vancehavner.com/biography/

## Permission Plan

Start with CLC Publications and ask for the correct rights and partnership contact for one
publisher-controlled Havner title. A pilot should separately define:

- title, edition, territory, term, and approved source files
- metadata, cover, description, and excerpt permissions
- purchaser-only reading and account entitlement
- full-text search and passage-linked indexing
- pricing, publisher and author share, reporting, refunds, support, and takedown
- audio, video, transcript, presentation, quotation, and narration rights, if any

Nothing should be presented as licensed, available for purchase, or endorsed until those terms are
received in writing.

## Final Recommendation

Publish the three original link labels in the rights manifest. Send the prepared CLC permissions
inquiry only after explicit approval. Keep all broader catalog and media uses blocked pending a
title-level agreement and theological review.
