## Verified Policy Boundaries

The official sharing page identifies audio sermons, video presentations,
O Timothy magazine, free eBooks, and website reports as shareable categories.
It also says that proper credit is required.

The free-eBook rule is narrower than general sharing language. The official
page says the files may be given to others intact, but they may not be posted
or distributed from another website or blog. The files must retain their text
and copyright notices, and readers should obtain current copies from Way of
Life because the books are updated.

The policy identifies these as non-shareable without separate permission:

- Fundamental Baptist Digital Library
- print editions
- paid eBooks
- paid or store videos
- PowerPoint presentations

The current free-eBook page still lists the five first-review candidates:

- Does Salvation Make a Difference?
- The Bible's Proof
- The Bible's Amazing Story
- Questions Answered on the Bible & Christianity
- The Bible Knowledge Test

The official contact page routes website, eBook, and download questions to
`support@wayoflife.org` and lists `fbns@wayoflife.org` as the general address.

## Conservative App Use Decision

Allowed now after title-level factual and doctrinal review:

- record title, author, resource type, and official source URL
- link users to the official Way of Life download or media page
- describe the public rights boundary without implying endorsement

Requires written permission or item-specific confirmation before public use:

- cover or promotional graphics
- excerpts from free eBooks
- inline embeds of official audio or video
- transcripts, captions, or derivative study notes based on copyrighted media
- public hosting or redistribution of any free-eBook file
- public hosting or redistribution of audio or video files
- AI narration or text-to-speech
- presentation export of copyrighted text, images, audio, or video
- paid-access inclusion, resale, affiliate use, or other commercial use

Not requested and not approved:

- paid books or eBooks
- Store media
- Fundamental Baptist Digital Library
- PowerPoint presentations
- broad catalog rights
