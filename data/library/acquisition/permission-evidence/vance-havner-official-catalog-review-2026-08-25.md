## Findings

The official Havner site offers *Havner's Reflections on Prophecy* through a free-book page and
links to an official store. Neither page supplies a license permitting another website or app to
copy, host, index, adapt, narrate, or sell the text. "Free" describes access from the official
source; it is not a public-domain or redistribution statement.

CLC Publications maintains a current Vance Havner catalog. Its author page identifies *Holy
Desperation* and *Reflections on the Gospels*, with print and eBook identifiers where applicable.
CLC also publishes an official permissions route. These findings establish source identity and a
contact path, not reuse rights.

The reviewed direct CLC URL guessed for *Reflections on the Gospels* returned 404 on 2026-08-25,
so the manifest uses the verified CLC author page instead of preserving an unverified product URL.

## Approved Public App Use

- Display an original factual title label.
- Link to the complete official page in a separate browser tab.
- Cite the official publisher or ministry.
- State accurately that broader access is pending permission.

## Not Approved

- Full-text or excerpt hosting, copying, indexing, search, or account unlocks.
- Book-cover, photograph, graphic, or publisher-description reuse.
- Audio, video, transcript, quotation collection, embed, download, or streaming.
- AI/TTS narration, adaptation, summarization from protected text, or derivative media.
- Paid access, subscription inclusion, resale, or implied partnership.
