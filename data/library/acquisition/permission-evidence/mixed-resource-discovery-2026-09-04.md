# Mixed study and teaching source-link review

Reviewed: 2026-09-04

Scope: nine official source-page listings only. No book text, quotations, poems, hymn lyrics, images, slides, or audio files were copied. No purchase, account creation, download agreement, or permission request was submitted. Link-only classification records the catalog's limited use; it is not a claim of a direct license from these publishers.

## Source findings

- [The Treasury of Rare Dispensational Commentaries](https://www.wayoflife.org/reports/treasury_of_rare_dispensational_comm.html): Historical report, not an available download. Consult current product pages; inclusion here does not approve redistribution of the publisher's compiled scans.
- [Baptist/Waldensian History Library](https://www.wayoflife.org/publications/baptist_history_library.php): Paid collection of 30 PDF books. Purchase and downloads remain on the publisher's site; no files acquired or copied.
- [Fundamental Baptist Digital Library — Discontinued](https://www.wayoflife.org/publications/fundamental_baptist_digital_library.php): Publisher explicitly says this item is no longer available. Do not present as an available purchase, free book, or downloadable collection.
- [The Preacher the Student](https://www.wayoflife.org/reports/the-preacher-the-student.php): Official article dated August 6, 2024. Historical anecdotes and quotations require checking their original sources before promotion into verified preaching helps.
- [Way of Life Conference, Preaching, and Teaching Audio](https://www.wayoflife.org/audiosermons/): The official page says its previous SermonAudio data feed is not working and directs listeners to SermonAudio. Directory link only: no individual tracks, playback, durations, or radio rotation verified.
- [Way of Life PowerPoint/Keynote Presentations](https://www.wayoflife.org/publications/powerpoint.php): Official slide-set directory. Each set needs its own terms and content review before download, image extraction, export, or redistribution. No slide files added.
- [FreeBibleimages — Bible Story Images](https://www.freebibleimages.org/): Teaching use is offered free, but reuse conditions vary by contributor and image set. Review each Terms of Download and attribution before app import or slide export. Captions may use other translations; any Scripture added to this app must be KJV.
- [Hymnary — Hymns and Hymnal Research](https://hymnary.org/): Research directory, not a blanket public-domain license. Lyrics, tunes, arrangements, scans, and recordings each require separate rights and accuracy review; no hymn text or recording copied.
- [Verses](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/77809): Official catalog identifies an 1894 New York edition and public-domain status in the USA. Link-only in this batch; no poem excerpts or text imported. Review liturgical and doctrinal context, exact wording, and applicable local rights before reuse.

## Rights boundary

The Way of Life sharing policy printed at the end of The Preacher the Student distinguishes shareable reports and free media from store products, specifically excluding the Fundamental Baptist Digital Library from copying/distribution. This batch uses citations and official links only, not that broader sharing permission. Its paid scan collections must not be treated as public domain merely because some original works are old.

FreeBibleimages describes free teaching use but requires each set's Terms of Download; contributor-specific reuse conditions remain unreviewed. Hymnary is a discovery source, not evidence that every hosted text, score, or recording is reusable. Project Gutenberg's Verses catalog identifies the 1894 edition as public domain in the USA; importing an edition or promoting excerpts remains a separate step.

The discontinued Digital Library is clearly labeled in its title and notes. The old commentary treasury report is historical guidance, not evidence of current product availability. The audio directory's current external-listening route is recorded without claiming in-app playback validation.

## Content safeguards

No secondhand quotation or historical anecdote from the preacher article was promoted as independently verified. No doctrinal endorsement of all contributors or books is implied. All Scripture wording subsequently imported into the app must be KJV. Image sets and poems require content-level review before presentation export or inclusion in verified preaching helps.
