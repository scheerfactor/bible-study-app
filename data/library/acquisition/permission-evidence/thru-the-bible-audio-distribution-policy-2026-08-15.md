## Rights Summary

Thru the Bible's published copyright notice permits and encourages reproduction and distribution of print or audio TTB material when all of these conditions are met:

- wording is not altered
- an excerpt is clearly identified as an excerpt
- no fee of any kind is charged for the copied material, including reproduction costs
- Dr. J. Vernon McGee and Thru the Bible are clearly attributed
- web-posted copies include the requested link to https://www.ttb.org

Required attribution:

> By Dr. J. Vernon McGee © Thru the Bible, www.ttb.org.

## App Decision

- Add official-link listings for the whole-Bible audio study and Sunday Sermons now.
- Keep every TTB link and copied resource outside paid or subscriber-only access.
- Do not alter audio, transcripts, or wording.
- Do not use the unrelated Archive.org upload as the source of truth.
- Before hosting audio, verify the exact official TTB source file, title, and attribution and retain a title-level intake record.
- Keep the earlier licensing request open for uses not covered by the public policy, including commercial bundles, derivative material, or unclear embedding arrangements.
