## Rights Finding

Both Library of Congress item pages identify their collection books as public
domain and free to use and reuse. The requested credit line is Library of
Congress. The app retains the item page, direct OCR URL, edition date, credit,
and checksum for each imported file.

## OCR Review

The LOC OCR wording was preserved without silently rewriting the source. Only
extra blank lines at the end of each downloaded file were normalized to one
final newline; source and imported checksums are recorded above. Beginning,
middle, and ending samples were inspected. Ordinary prose and poetry are
generally readable, but decorative title pages, contents, running heads, and
some dates contain obvious OCR defects. Both records therefore carry an OCR
spot-check warning and direct readers to compare the page scan before quotation
or public teaching.

## Scope Boundary

This review covers only the two identified print editions and their LOC scans.
It does not establish rights for:

- modern editions, annotations, covers, or editorial work;
- hymn tunes not printed in these editions;
- later arrangements, orchestrations, or presentation graphics;
- historical or modern audio and video recordings; or
- content hosted by Wholesome Words or another third-party website.

Any tune, arrangement, recording, portrait, or separate hymn publication still
requires its own source and rights record.
