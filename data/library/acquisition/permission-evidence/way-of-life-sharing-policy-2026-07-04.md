## Policy Summary

Way of Life Literature states that its material falls into two categories: shareable and non-shareable.

Shareable categories named by the policy include:

- audio sermons
- O Timothy magazine
- FBIS articles
- free eVideos
- free eBooks
- parts of reports or entire reports, when proper credit/citation is given

The policy asks that links to original reports be included where possible because reports may be updated or expanded.

Non-shareable categories named by the policy include:

- Store items
- Fundamental Baptist Digital Library
- print editions of books
- electronic editions of books they sell
- videos they sell

Questions are directed to `support@wayoflife.org`.

## App Use Decision

The Bible Study App may create review records for Way of Life shareable categories, but each item must be reviewed before public display.

Allowed after item-level review:

- list official metadata for shareable items
- link to official Way of Life pages
- quote or display report/article excerpts when citation and source link are included
- include shareable audio sermons, O Timothy, FBIS articles, free eVideos, and free eBooks only when the item is clearly in a shareable category

Not allowed without separate written permission:

- importing Store items
- importing or redistributing the Fundamental Baptist Digital Library
- hosting paid print-book or ebook text
- hosting paid videos
- treating this policy as broad catalog permission
- AI/TTS narration, AI training, paid subscription inclusion, or offline full-library redistribution

## Required Guardrails

- Keep all Way of Life Store and paid resources marked `Permission Needed`.
- Add source URL, citation, and rights note to every approved shareable item.
- Use official links whenever possible.
- Do not imply that Way of Life endorsed the app unless written permission says so.
- Continue waiting for direct email response before broader app integration.
