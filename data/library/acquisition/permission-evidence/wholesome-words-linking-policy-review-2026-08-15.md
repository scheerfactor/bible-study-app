## Policy Findings

The current terms welcome links from other websites without permission when the
link opens the complete Wholesome Words page in a non-framed browser window.
The terms reserve site text, images, photographs, code, and design, and require
written item-level permission before content is reused in another electronic
format or website. Permission is not offered for the catalog as a whole.

## Approved Public App Use

- Display an original, factual link label written for this app.
- Open the complete source page in a separate browser tab.
- Cite Wholesome Words as the source site.
- Keep separately verified public-domain Library books under their own source
  and rights records.

## Not Approved

- Copying biography text, quotations, timelines, family details, or lists from
  Wholesome Words into the app.
- Copying portraits, photographs, book-cover images, or other graphics.
- Copying or embedding HTML eBooks, audio, video, PDFs, or other linked files.
- Framing a Wholesome Words page inside the app.
- Creating AI/TTS narration, summaries derived from protected site text, or
  other derivative content from the site.
- Including Wholesome Words content in paid access.

## Duplicate And Gap Review

The verified public-domain manifest already contains six Gipsy Smith records,
eight Lewis Sperry Chafer records, and seven William Carey records. These new
records are discovery links, not duplicate book imports. Fanny Crosby and M. R.
DeHaan had no matching public-domain book records in the manifest at review
time, so they remain link-only discovery gaps until exact title-level rights are
verified.

## Public-Launch Decision

Publish the six link records only. Do not copy source content or imply that
Wholesome Words approved, sponsored, or licensed the app. Request written
permission separately before any biography excerpt, portrait, audio, embedded
resource, or other electronic reuse.
