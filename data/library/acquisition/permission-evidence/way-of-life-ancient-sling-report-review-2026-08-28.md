This report is itself described as an excerpt from a Store book. The reviewed
public report may fall within the credited report-sharing permission, but that

## Conservative App Decision

The first app release uses:

- accurate title, author, publisher, and date metadata;
- a citation and official report link;
- passage-indexed discovery for the listed Bible references;
- a factual label identifying it as archaeology and historical background.

The app does not copy:

- the report text;
- the page image or illustration;
- quotations or measurements attributed to third-party publications;
- text or media from the underlying Store book;
- any KJV verse text.

Before any report excerpt or complete report is hosted, complete a separate
editorial review of attribution, third-party quotation scope, image rights,
KJV wording, update tracking, and presentation/export behavior. Paid access,
resale, AI/TTS narration, and derivative media remain outside the reviewed
scope.
