# R. G. Lee — *Payday Someday* Rights Review

Reviewed: 2026-08-30

## Decision

**Acquisition candidate only. Nothing from this title is approved for public display, playback, transcription, presentation reuse, narration, download, or sale.**

## Evidence

- B&H Publishing Group published a 1998, 280-page edition titled *Payday Someday*, ISBN 9780805418651.
- B&H’s official contact page directs questions about using, republishing, reproducing, or copyright to its permissions form.
- Multiple sermon platforms list recordings, but those public pages do not document an app redistribution license from the original recording rightsholder.
- The text edition, original recording, transcript, platform embed, cover, photographs, and any new narration are separate assets and may have different owners.

## Approved Internal Actions

- retain factual bibliographic metadata in the private acquisition workspace
- contact B&H through its official permissions route
- ask B&H or Bellevue Baptist Church who controls the original recording master
- prepare a narrow one-title proposal
- record any reply and its exact scope

## Prohibited Until Written Permission

- copying the sermon text or transcript into the app
- streaming, downloading, clipping, or scheduling a third-party recording in Radio
- generating a new reading with AI/TTS or a human narrator
- using sermon quotations on presentation slides beyond separately reviewed legal limits
- using a cover or photograph without an approved asset source
- placing any permitted free copy behind paid access
- implying endorsement by R. G. Lee, his estate, Bellevue Baptist Church, B&H, or a recording platform

## First Outreach Questions

1. Does B&H control digital/app permissions for the 1998 edition?
2. May the app display the title, author, factual description, one supplied cover image, and an official purchase link?
3. Is one short approved excerpt available for a free public beta listing?
4. Who controls the original sermon audio and transcript?
5. Who can discuss future full-text, searchable transcript, narration, presentation, and paid-access licensing?
