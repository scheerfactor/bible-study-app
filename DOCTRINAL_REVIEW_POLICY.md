# Doctrinal Review Policy

Father's Business Bible Study must not generate doctrine automatically. Study entries, library resources, commentary notes, people/place connections, types of Christ, prophecy connections, and warnings must be manually curated and reviewed.

## Purpose

The app should help users understand the Bible while keeping Scripture central. It should support a conservative Baptist-friendly perspective without carelessly importing material that confuses, weakens, or contradicts the faith.

## Review Principles

1. Scripture is primary.
2. Use KJV wording for Scripture references and quotations.
3. Do not present speculative interpretations as settled doctrine.
4. Clearly label historical, devotional, commentary, and doctrinal material.
5. Do not include a resource merely because it is public domain.
6. Record concerns honestly.
7. Keep review notes visible to maintainers and eventually summarized to users.

## Required Review Fields

Each resource or curated entry should include:

- reviewed_by
- reviewed_at
- review_status
- perspective_notes
- doctrinal_concerns
- recommended_use
- user_warning
- source_references

## Review Status

- `approved`: Suitable for current app use.
- `approved with caution`: Useful but needs a visible note or limited placement.
- `needs review`: Not yet cleared.
- `do not import`: Not suitable or rights/doctrine concerns are unresolved.

## Types of Christ Policy

Types of Christ must be manually reviewed. Do not auto-generate typology.

Each type must include:

- Old Testament reference
- description
- why it points to Christ
- New Testament fulfillment or connection reference
- review notes

Avoid:

- Overstating typology
- Building doctrine from imagination
- Treating every similarity as a type
- Using unsourced claims without Scripture anchors

## Prophecy Connection Policy

Prophecy connections must include:

- Old Testament reference
- New Testament fulfillment or connection reference
- short explanation
- review status
- notes on disputed or partial fulfillment if relevant

Avoid:

- Automatically connecting passages by keywords only
- Presenting disputed interpretations without warning
- Mixing devotional language with textual claims without labels

## Commentary Policy

Commentaries may be useful, but they must not replace Scripture.

For each commentary entry:

- Verify rights.
- Record author and resource title.
- Record public-domain status.
- Keep excerpts short or import only verified permissible full text.
- Label doctrinal concerns.
- Keep commentary visually secondary to the Bible text.

## Library Warning Examples

Use plain labels such as:

- "Baptist history resource"
- "Devotional classic; use with discernment"
- "Useful historical work; not all doctrine endorsed"
- "Needs doctrinal review before import"
- "Do not import yet"

## AI and Automation Boundaries

AI may help organize, format, summarize candidate notes, or find missing metadata, but AI must not be the final doctrinal authority.

AI must not:

- Create doctrine automatically.
- Invent types of Christ.
- Invent prophecy fulfillment.
- Mark resources approved.
- Rewrite Scripture.
- Remove doctrinal warnings.

Human review is required before content becomes trusted study data.

## Beta Rule

During private beta, prefer a smaller reviewed set of resources and entries over a large impressive library. It is better for the app to be trustworthy and useful than broad and confusing.
