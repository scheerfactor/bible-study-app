# Private Beta Tester Instructions

Thank you for testing Father's Business Bible Study. Please use the app normally and report anything confusing, broken, slow, or unfinished.

Production app:

https://bible-study-app-eight.vercel.app/

## First Five Minutes

1. Open the app on your phone.
2. Start on Today.
3. Confirm the Daily Growth Dashboard shows Smart Resume and Ministry Dashboard.
4. Use the Daily Growth Dashboard:
   - Read
   - Listen
   - Journal
   - Pray
   - Memorize
   - Continue
5. Refresh the page and confirm your local progress did not disappear.
6. Use Give feedback if anything feels unclear.

## Bible Reader Test

1. Open Bible.
2. Quick Jump to `John 3:16`.
3. Tap John 3:16.
4. Try Study, Dictionary, Cross References, Notes, Audio, Commentary, and Memory.
5. Highlight the verse.
6. Add a note.
7. Bookmark the verse.
8. Refresh and confirm the data remains.

Also test:

- `Romans 8:28`
- `Luke 24`
- `Amos 1`
- Previous and Next chapter buttons
- Recent passages
- Favorites
- Markers A-D

## Daily Growth Test

1. On Today, mark Bible reading complete.
2. Open Proverb of the Day.
3. Open Prayer from the dashboard.
4. Open Journal from the dashboard and save an entry.
5. Review the memory verse.
6. Open Continue Library Reading.
7. Open Study Playlist.
8. Confirm the progress cards update clearly.

## Prayer Test

1. Add a prayer request.
2. Add a missionary request if possible.
3. Mark prayed today.
4. Mark answered.
5. Export prayer list.
6. Refresh and confirm the prayer entry remains.

## Journal Test

1. Start today's journal entry.
2. Add words to define.
3. Write a short application.
4. Save the entry.
5. Export the entry.
6. Export devotional notes.
7. Refresh and confirm the entry remains.

## Library Test

1. Open Library.
2. Search for an author or title.
3. Open a resource.
4. Read part of it.
5. Try Listen, Stop, speed, and sleep timer where available.
6. Add a note or bookmark.
7. Refresh and confirm progress remains.

## Teaching And Passage Study Test

1. Open Passage Guide for John 3.
2. Open Teaching Workspace.
3. Try John 3, Romans 8, Luke 24, and Amos 1.
4. Export teaching notes.
5. Use the connected workflow buttons to send study material into a sermon.
6. Report anything that feels cluttered or hard to understand.

## Sermon And Slides Test

1. Open Sermons.
2. Load the John 3 sample sermon.
3. Open Builder and export sermon markdown.
4. Export preaching notes.
5. Open Slides.
6. Generate or review the slide outline.
7. Copy the slide outline.
8. Download the slide outline Markdown.
9. Enter Present mode and exit.
10. Report any slide text, layout, or mobile issue.

## What Not To Test Yet

- Payments or purchases.
- AI tools.
- Public uploads.
- Licensed audio.
- Full church management.
- PDF, Keynote, Proclaim, and remote presentation control.

## Feedback Questions

- What worked smoothly?
- What confused you?
- Was the Bible reader easy to use on your phone?
- Did Today help you know what to do next?
- Did the Journal feel useful?
- Did Prayer feel simple and reverent?
- Was Library reading/listening comfortable?
- Did any page feel slow?
- Did anything look unfinished without a clear label?
- What should be fixed before inviting more testers?
