# Public Launch, Pricing, and Sustainable Growth Plan

## Recommendation

Use a free-core, paid-workflow model.

- Keep KJV Bible reading, core study tools, and reviewed public-domain resources free.
- Add optional support before adding a paywall.
- Charge for ministry time saved: advanced sermon preparation, presentation tools, exports, storage, original media, and church collaboration.
- Sell modern books or media only through official outbound purchase links or a written commercial license.
- Keep licensed resource packs separate from the normal subscription unless the agreement explicitly permits subscription access.

This gives the app a clear reason to exist: move quickly from a KJV passage to trustworthy study, organized teaching, and a usable presentation without opening many disconnected tools.

## What Makes It Worth Paying For

People should not have to pay merely to read Scripture or access public-domain text. They may pay when the app repeatedly saves time and reduces friction.

The strongest paid workflow is:

1. Open a KJV passage.
2. Define words with Webster and Strong's.
3. Compare commentary, cross references, maps, people, places, and themes.
4. Save conclusions and applications.
5. Build a sermon or lesson outline.
6. Turn the outline into readable slides.
7. Present with notes, timer, remote control, and church media.
8. Archive and reuse the study later.

That passage-to-presentation workflow is more defensible than charging for a large pile of books.

## Recommended Plans

Prices are pilot hypotheses, not permanent promises. Test them with real users before building complicated billing logic.

| Plan | Pilot price | Purpose | Included value |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Scripture and serious basic study | KJV reader, basic search, Webster, Strong's, TSK, public-domain commentary and books, bookmarks, favorites, notes, reading progress, basic sermon notes, basic present mode |
| Supporter | Choose amount or $5/month | Voluntary support without feature pressure | Everything in Free, supporter recognition if desired, development updates, no exclusive Scripture or public-domain content |
| Ministry Pro | $9/month or $90/year | Personal study and teaching productivity | Advanced study sessions, sermon archive, reusable templates, advanced search of personal work, richer exports, private uploads, larger storage, presentation history, original study packs |
| Pastor and Presentation | $15/month or $150/year | Weekly preaching and teaching | Everything in Pro, advanced slide builder, PowerPoint/PDF export when ready, remote control, countdowns, confidence view, church branding, sermon media library, additional storage |
| Church Team pilot | $29/month or $290/year | Small ministry team | Up to 5 seats, shared service plans, approved team media, shared sermon/lesson folders, presentation handoff, role-based access, consolidated billing |

Do not launch all plans at once. Start with Free, Supporter, and one Pro beta plan. Split Pro and Pastor only after usage shows two distinct audiences.

## One-Time Purchase Options

A lifetime promise is risky when sync, storage, bandwidth, support, and licensed media create continuing costs. One-time purchases work best for items with bounded support costs.

Good one-time products later:

- Public-Domain Library Edition: $49-$79 for an offline/local collection without unlimited cloud services
- Founding Pro license: $99-$149, limited quantity, with fair-use cloud limits and no promise of future licensed packs
- Original sermon-theme packs: $7-$19
- Original church presentation packs: $9-$29
- Original map, timeline, or teaching-image packs: $9-$29
- Publisher-licensed book or commentary packs: price set per agreement

Avoid an unlimited lifetime cloud plan. If a founding lifetime offer is tested, state that third-party licensed resources, metered AI/media generation, and unusually large storage are separate.

## Donation and Support Path

The safest early money path is a simple hosted support link before a custom subscription system.

Suggested wording:

> Father's Business Bible Study is being built to help Christians read, study, obey, teach, and preach the Scriptures. If this tool helps you, you may support continued development. Core Bible reading and public-domain study resources remain free.

Rules:

- Do not interrupt Bible reading with donation prompts.
- Do not make a gift appear spiritually required.
- Do not promise tax deductibility unless the recipient is legally qualified and the claim has been reviewed.
- If the project is not operating through a qualified charitable organization, use "support," "tip," or "contribution" and clearly say it is not tax-deductible.
- Keep supporter payments separate from purchases that deliver specific paid value.

Stripe Payment Links can support one-time payments, subscriptions, and customer-chosen amounts without building checkout first. Current standard US card pricing and Billing fees must be included in margin calculations. See [Stripe Payment Links](https://stripe.com/payments/payment-links), [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), and [Stripe Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing).

The IRS says charitable deductions generally require a contribution to a qualified organization. See [IRS charitable contribution deductions](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contribution-deductions).

## Books and Commentary Commerce

Use three separate models. Never blur them.

### 1. Official Store Links

Start here for copyrighted books.

- Show reviewed metadata, cover art, descriptions, and excerpts only within granted scope.
- Link to the author's or publisher's official store.
- Add an affiliate code only after a written affiliate agreement.
- Do not copy the paid ebook, audio, or video into the app.
- Do not imply that link permission grants subscription or resale rights.

This is the fastest and lowest-risk way to help authors sell books while making the app more useful.

### 2. Licensed Add-On Packs

Use this after a rights holder grants digital distribution rights.

- License specific titles, editions, formats, territories, and platforms.
- Record whether search, quotation, offline use, audio, TTS, presentation export, and paid access are allowed.
- Agree on wholesale price or revenue share, reporting, refunds, updates, attribution, and termination.
- Sell the pack separately so the customer understands what is being purchased.
- Preserve access rules if the agreement ends; do not promise perpetual access unless the contract permits it.

Possible packs:

- Whole-Bible commentary set
- Baptist doctrine and distinctives collection
- Pastoral ministry collection
- Prayer and devotional collection
- Christian school and education collection
- Evangelism and missions collection
- KJV history and defense collection
- Author collection

### 3. Marketplace Later

Do not build a multi-publisher marketplace during beta. A marketplace adds seller onboarding, tax, payouts, refunds, disputes, reporting, and merchant-of-record questions. Stripe Connect can route marketplace payments, but it does not replace publisher contracts or product-rights review. See [Stripe Connect for marketplaces](https://stripe.com/connect/marketplaces).

## Presentation as the Best Premium Feature

Presentation tools are original app functionality, so they are a cleaner paid value than repackaging free books.

Keep free:

- basic sermon-to-slide outline
- KJV Scripture slides
- simple title, point, application, and closing slides
- basic present mode

Make Pro when reliable:

- PowerPoint and PDF export
- reusable sermon and series themes
- church logo and brand presets
- timer, countdown, and time-remaining view
- confidence monitor and next-slide preview
- phone/tablet remote control
- service announcements and phones-on-silent slides
- location, date, speaker, and event presets
- presentation archive and duplication
- team handoff and shared media
- advanced image positioning and accessibility checks

The app should sell polish, speed, storage, and coordination. It should not sell KJV verse text itself.

## Sermon Themes, Images, Quotes, and Illustrations

### Original Theme Packs

Create visually distinct, reusable packs for:

- revival and evangelism
- missions
- prayer
- prophecy
- family and Christian home
- youth and Christian school
- Baptist history
- Bible geography
- Christmas and resurrection services
- funerals, weddings, and special services

Each pack should include background images, title layouts, Scripture layouts, quote layouts, lower thirds, countdown slides, and matching announcement slides.

### Image Sources

Only ship images that are:

- original photography or illustration
- commissioned with written commercial rights
- verified public domain
- CC0
- properly licensed for the exact commercial and presentation use
- generated specifically for the app with retained provenance and human review

Historical maps and archaeology images need source, creator, date, rights basis, modifications, and a careful note separating evidence from interpretation.

### Quotes and Sermon Illustrations

- Prefer public-domain quotations, Scripture, permission-granted excerpts, and original writing.
- Record exact author, work, edition, page or timestamp, and rights status.
- Do not assume a short modern quotation is automatically safe for a commercial database.
- Require source review for funny stories, missionary accounts, and evangelist illustrations.
- Label uncertain anecdotes and do not present them as historical fact.
- Let users save private illustrations from their own ministry without publishing them globally.

## Content Expansion Priorities

Content should deepen passage usefulness, not merely increase the item count.

### Priority 1: Passage Depth

- Fill thin commentary coverage in books and chapters with fewer useful notes.
- Add chapter and verse links to existing books, sermons, maps, and media.
- Continue Webster OCR overlays by KJV use.
- Expand Strong's gloss quality and TSK depth.
- Build reviewed people, places, events, doctrines, and Bible-theme connections.

### Priority 2: Preaching and Teaching

- Index public-domain sermons by passage, doctrine, occasion, and audience.
- Add sourced outlines, applications, questions, quotations, and illustrations.
- Build original sermon and lesson templates.
- Add Christian school, Sunday school, discipleship, and seminary reading paths.
- Create preaching-help pages for introductions, conclusions, invitations, transitions, and applications.

### Priority 3: Visual Study

- Expand verified historical maps and geography.
- Add Bible objects, customs, plants, animals, measurements, and architecture.
- Add archaeology exhibits with careful claims and citations.
- Add timelines, family trees, journeys, and event sequences.
- Connect every visual to relevant KJV passages.

### Priority 4: Audio and Video

- Add official hosted links or embeds first.
- Rehost only when written permission or clear public-domain rights allow it.
- Add chapter markers and verified timestamps.
- Separate text, audio, video, transcript, TTS, presentation, and paid-access rights.
- Use original teaching videos only after editorial and doctrinal review.

### Priority 5: Hymns and Worship Presentation

- Add public-domain hymn text with source and rights evidence.
- Add verified public-domain or licensed piano/MIDI arrangements.
- Support lyrics, instrumental playback, service order, and presentation slides.
- Keep modern hymn lyrics and recordings blocked until separately licensed.

## App Store and Web Payment Constraints

Launch payments on the web first. A later native app needs its own payment review.

- Apple generally requires In-App Purchase for digital features or content unlocked in an App Store app. See [Apple App Review Guideline 3.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- Google Play generally requires Play Billing for in-app digital features, subscriptions, and content, subject to regional programs and exceptions. See [Google Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) and [service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622).
- Do not assume a Stripe web checkout can be placed unchanged inside a future iOS or Android app.
- Keep entitlements provider-neutral so web, Apple, and Google purchases can map to the same internal plans where platform rules permit.

## Launch Sequence

### Stage 1: Free Beta

- Invite users into the current free app.
- Measure whether they return weekly to read, study, save, teach, or present.
- Fix reliability, mobile usability, sync, export, and rights labeling.
- Do not advertise a large future marketplace.

### Stage 2: Optional Support

- Add one hosted support link.
- Offer one-time choose-your-amount support and an optional $5 monthly supporter level.
- Give appreciation, not exclusive Scripture access.
- Publish simple use-of-funds language.

### Stage 3: One Paid Beta

- Offer one $9/month or $90/year Ministry Pro plan.
- Include only features that already work reliably.
- Grandfather early users for a defined period, not forever without limits.
- Interview cancellations and non-conversions.

### Stage 4: Presentation and Church Plans

- Split Pastor and Presentation from Pro only after weekly presentation use is proven.
- Pilot church teams with a small number of congregations.
- Add seat management, shared folders, permissions, backups, and support before broad sales.

### Stage 5: Licensed Packs

- Begin with one cooperative author or publisher and a small title set.
- Test reporting, refunds, access, attribution, and support.
- Expand only after the first agreement works cleanly.

## Viability Measures

Do not judge viability by resource count alone.

Track:

- activation: user completes a meaningful Bible study action on the first visit
- weekly retention: user returns to read, study, prepare, or present
- study completion: passage opened, tools used, and notes saved
- teaching completion: outline or presentation created and reused
- time saved: users report that the app replaces multiple steps or tools
- support conversion: active free users who voluntarily contribute
- paid conversion: active users who choose Pro
- paid retention: subscribers still active after 3 and 6 months
- rights health: zero public leaks of blocked or unclear content
- support burden: hours of support per paying account

Suggested gates before normal paid launch:

- at least 25 active beta testers
- at least 10 users returning weekly for four weeks
- at least 5 users completing sermon, lesson, or presentation workflows
- at least 5 willing paid-pilot users
- tested cancellation, refund, export, and account deletion paths
- written privacy policy, terms, support process, and rights separation

5. Build a rights-aware resource entitlement model for future licensed packs.

7. Add passage links to the strongest books, sermons, maps, hymns, and media already present.

10. Interview pastors, teachers, and serious students about the one workflow they would pay to save each week.

## Decision

The recommended first public model is:

- Free core Bible study
- Optional support
- One Ministry Pro beta plan after retention is proven
- Presentation tools as the main premium value
- One-time original media and theme packs
- Official publisher links before licensed book sales
- Separately licensed resource packs later

This is more viable, more trustworthy, and easier to explain than either making everything free forever or putting the entire library behind one subscription.
