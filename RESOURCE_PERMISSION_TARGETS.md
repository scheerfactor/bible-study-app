# Resource Permission Targets

## Purpose

This list identifies authors, ministries, publishers, and resource holders that may fit Father's Business Bible Study after theological, rights, and quality review.

Do not add broad publishers or authors automatically. Every author, ministry, book, sermon, audio file, video, commentary, article, or excerpt must be reviewed before being marked safe for the app.

## Safety Rule

Do not send mass emails automatically. Prepare drafts only. Stephen must review each contact and approve each email before it is sent.

## Review Priority

The app should remain:

- KJV-friendly
- Baptist-friendly
- dispensational-friendly or compatible
- clear on salvation by grace through faith
- careful with doctrinal differences
- rights-first and permission-first

## First Contact Strategy

Start with reachable and ministry-aligned contacts:

1. Known local pastors and teachers.
2. Ministries with direct contact paths.
3. Authors/publishers where one exact title is selected.
4. Public-domain-adjacent works requiring edition confirmation.
5. Larger publishers only after screenshots, demo video, and beta proof are ready.

## First Ask

Ask for narrow permission first:

- listing metadata and official links
- one approved excerpt
- one sample chapter
- one sermon or lesson
- audio/video embedding only if officially allowed
- future full digital or paid access discussion later

## Do Not Do

- Do not ask for whole catalogs first.
- Do not imply endorsement by the author or ministry.
- Do not publish copyrighted text without written permission.
- Do not treat online availability as permission.
- Do not treat physical ownership as digital distribution rights.
- Do not combine text, audio, video, chart, image, and transcript rights into one assumption.
