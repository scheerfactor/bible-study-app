# Publisher And Author Discovery Interview

Prepared 2026-08-22. This interview collects product feedback. It does not grant rights, approve content, create a partnership, or change an existing license.

## Purpose

Learn what trusted authors, publishers, pastors, teachers, schools, and ministries would need before recommending or licensing resources to Father's Business Bible Study.

Use a 20-30 minute conversation when possible. Record the respondent's words accurately and separate product feedback from permission decisions.

## Organization

1. What ministry, publisher, school, or catalog do you represent?
2. Which audiences do you serve: pastors, teachers, students, churches, schools, seminaries, families, missionaries, or general readers?
3. Who controls digital text, ebook, audio, video, image, excerpt, translation, classroom, and sales rights?
4. Are any rights controlled by a different author, estate, narrator, photographer, broadcaster, or publisher?

## Most Useful Resources

1. Which 5-20 titles or series would be most useful to serious Bible students?
2. Which resources are best for verse-by-verse study, doctrine, preaching, Sunday School, missions, prayer, Christian living, education, or devotional reading?
3. Which titles should never be separated from a complete volume, series, or edition?
4. What editions, corrections, disclaimers, or doctrinal context must remain visible?

## Bible Study Workflow

1. How should a resource appear beside a KJV verse or passage?
2. May headings, paragraphs, or notes be linked to books, chapters, verses, topics, people, places, and Strong's numbers?
3. What search-result snippet length would be acceptable?
4. Should users be able to search across every purchased title or only within one book?
5. Which private highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading plans, sermon notes, lesson notes, and cross-resource links are useful?
6. What would help a pastor or teacher move from reading to a faithful sermon, lesson, or presentation more quickly?

## Audio And Video

1. Do you control existing audiobook, sermon, course, radio, podcast, or video rights?
2. Would you permit official links, embeds, direct streams, protected rehosting, downloads, or offline playback?
3. May transcripts be displayed, searched, quoted, or linked to passages?
4. Are browser text-to-speech, generated narration, and human narration separate permissions?
5. What reporting, attribution, editing, advertising, and paid-access restrictions are required?

## Commercial Models

1. Would you prefer publisher checkout with a verified app unlock?
2. Would you consider app marketplace sales with a publisher-majority revenue share?
3. Would you consider individual, family, church, school, seminary, or ministry subscriptions?
4. Do you prefer wholesale pricing, per-sale royalties, per-seat licensing, a minimum guarantee, a usage pool, referral fees, or another arrangement?
5. Who should handle taxes, refunds, chargebacks, customer support, and file updates?
6. What sales statements, usage reports, payment schedule, and audit rights would you need?

## Trust And Recommendation

1. What would make you comfortable recommending this app to pastors, teachers, and students?
2. What content, doctrinal, privacy, security, accessibility, or customer-service failures would prevent a recommendation?
3. How should the app distinguish Scripture, publisher text, historical resources, editorial labels, and user notes?
4. What correction, review, update, withdrawal, and emergency takedown controls should a partner have?
5. What does existing Bible software make too slow, complicated, expensive, or difficult for your readers?
6. Which single workflow should Father's Business do exceptionally well?

## Close

Ask permission to:

- retain the interview notes privately
- contact the respondent with a summary for correction
- invite the respondent to a private product demonstration
- send a separate title-level or catalog-level licensing proposal

Do not treat an interview answer as permission to copy, display, host, sell, stream, quote, narrate, or index any resource.
