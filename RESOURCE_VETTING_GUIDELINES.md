# Resource Vetting Guidelines

## Purpose

Father's Business Bible Study should be a curated KJV-friendly, Baptist-friendly, dispensational-friendly resource library. Resources may be useful even when not every doctrinal detail is endorsed, but users should not be left confused about the perspective of a resource.

Every author and resource must be reviewed before being marked safe for the app.

## Review Labels

### Approved Core

Use for resources that are a strong fit for normal recommendations.

Expected traits:

- KJV-friendly
- Baptist-friendly
- dispensational or compatible
- clear Gospel by grace through faith
- sound practical doctrine
- suitable for new believers, teachers, and pastors

### Approved With Note

Use for generally helpful resources with a visible note about perspective or limitations.

Examples:

- helpful but not Baptist
- useful historical or devotional value
- some denominational differences
- older wording or dated cultural references
- solid on the passage but not a core doctrinal recommendation

### Historical / Use Carefully

Use for resources that are valuable for history, quotations, comparison, or background, but should not be treated as primary doctrinal guidance.

Examples:

- replacement theology or covenant theology perspectives
- paedobaptist perspectives
- denominational assumptions that differ from Baptist doctrine
- older polemical works
- mixed doctrinal usefulness

### Needs Review

Default label for every new author, ministry, publisher, book, commentary, sermon, article, image, audio file, video, or study note until reviewed.

Use when:

- rights are not verified
- theology fit is unknown
- edition is unclear
- source quality is unclear
- OCR quality is questionable
- the resource has not been read or spot-checked

### Do Not Include

Use when a resource should not be included publicly in the app.

Reasons may include:

- false gospel
- cult or false religion material
- liberal/progressive theology presented as recommended study
- anti-KJV bias in core recommendations
- Roman Catholic doctrine promoted as doctrinal guidance
- sacramentalism or works-based salvation
- charismatic excess presented as normative doctrine
- unclear or denied rights
- plagiarized or unreliable source
- poor OCR quality that cannot be trusted

## Theological Review Criteria

Evaluate every resource for:

- KJV-friendly posture
- Baptist-friendly doctrine and church practice
- dispensational or compatible Bible interpretation
- clear Gospel by grace through faith
- avoids Lordship salvation confusion
- avoids sacramentalism
- avoids Catholic doctrine
- avoids liberal/progressive theology
- avoids charismatic excess
- avoids replacement theology unless clearly labeled as historical
- avoids anti-KJV bias in core recommendations

## Rights Review Criteria

Before public inclusion, confirm:

- title
- author
- publisher
- edition
- publication date
- source URL
- public-domain status or permission status
- whether text, images, charts, audio, video, and transcripts have separate rights
- whether free beta, free public access, paid access, TTS, and audiobook uses are allowed

## Quality Review Criteria

Check:

- source reliability
- OCR quality
- missing pages or chapters
- broken paragraphs
- obvious word-splitting errors
- title/author accuracy
- duplicate editions
- whether it should be grouped under an existing work
- whether it is safe for quotation

## Recommendation Rules

Only `Approved Core` resources should appear as default recommendations for new believers.

`Approved With Note` resources may appear with a short label.

`Historical / Use Carefully` resources should be available for mature study but not treated as primary guidance.

`Needs Review` resources should remain hidden from normal public recommendations.

`Do Not Include` resources should not appear publicly.

## Labels For User-Facing Notes

Use short, plain labels:

- Recommended
- Use with discernment
- Historical value
- Not all doctrine endorsed
- Baptist history
- Devotional classic
- Public-domain edition
- OCR spot-check needed
- Review before quoting
