# Publisher Catalog Partnership Proposal

Prepared 2026-08-22. This is a negotiation draft, not a license, offer, or authorization to publish copyrighted content.

## Vision

Father's Business Bible Study is building a KJV-centered, conservative Baptist-friendly digital library for pastors, teachers, missionaries, evangelists, Christian schools, and serious Bible students.

The goal is to make carefully reviewed books useful where study happens. With written permission, a purchased title could open beside the KJV passage, contribute publisher-approved notes to the relevant verse or passage, appear in library and topic search, and support private highlights, notes, sermon preparation, and lesson preparation.

We are seeking multi-title relationships with trusted authors, ministries, and publishers. A small pilot can establish accuracy and trust, while the agreement provides a path for an approved catalog to grow.

## Partnership Options

### 1. Publisher Sells, App Unlocks

- The publisher controls checkout and remains the seller.
- A verified purchase or publisher-issued code creates the reader's app entitlement.
- The publisher receives the sale and pays an agreed platform or referral fee.
- The agreement defines fulfillment, refunds, support, reporting, and entitlement restoration.

### 2. App Sells, Publisher Receives Royalty

- Father's Business provides checkout and the in-app digital unlock.
- The rights holder receives the agreed majority share of defined net receipts.
- The agreement defines merchant-of-record, payment fees, taxes, refunds, disputes, statements, and payout timing.
- A possible opening discussion range is 75-85% of defined net receipts to the rights holder and 15-25% to the platform. Final terms must be written and may differ by partner or title.

### 3. Licensed Subscription, Church, Or School Access

- Selected titles may be included in an individual, family, church, ministry, school, or seminary plan.
- Compensation may be per active seat, per title, by usage pool, by minimum guarantee, or by another written formula.
- A subscription license does not automatically grant permanent ownership, offline access, classroom copies, or presentation export.

### 4. Discovery And Official Links

- Reviewed titles may be listed with approved metadata, cover art, samples, and official links.
- This can begin before full-text licensing is complete.
- Listing or linking does not grant hosting, searching, passage linking, sale, or entitlement rights.

## Requested Digital Uses

Each partner may approve or decline these separately:

- secure full-text storage and display
- per-user paid access and cross-device restoration
- full-book search and short search-result snippets
- publisher-reviewed Bible verse, passage, topic, and Strong's links
- private notes, highlights, bookmarks, and reading progress
- limited quotations into private sermon or lesson work, within stated limits
- approved excerpts, descriptions, cover art, and promotional screenshots
- offline reading, if authorized
- church, school, seminary, classroom, or group licensing
- text-to-speech or human-narrated audio only under separate permission

The app will not claim AI training, generated summaries, audio rights, presentation export, broad classroom copying, or resale rights unless the agreement explicitly includes them.

## Publisher Protections

- Title and edition-level rights records
- Publisher approval of attribution and initial presentation
- Encrypted licensed storage outside public repositories
- Server-enforced customer entitlements
- Sales, refunds, royalties, and access records
- Agreed royalty statements and payment schedule
- Correction, update, withdrawal, and takedown process
- No implied rights for a sequel, new edition, audiobook, cover, or other format
- Clear handling of previously purchased access after termination

## Proposed Pilot

1. The publisher identifies 5-20 suitable catalog candidates.
2. Both parties select 1-3 pilot titles.
3. Rights, files, metadata, review, price, and reporting terms are completed for each pilot edition.
4. The publisher reviews the reader, search snippets, passage links, attribution, and store listing.
5. A private test verifies checkout, unlock, restoration, refunds, reporting, and content protection.
6. The pilot opens publicly only after written approval.
7. Additional approved titles follow the agreement's catalog expansion process.

## Information Needed From A Publisher

- rights-holder and contract contact
- title list, editions, ISBNs, and territorial rights
- available EPUB, HTML, XML, PDF, cover, metadata, and sample files
- preferred sales model and price control
- royalty, wholesale, minimum-guarantee, or referral expectations
- permitted search, passage-linking, note, quotation, offline, church, and school uses
- required attribution, doctrinal/editorial cautions, and marketing review
- file update, correction, customer-support, and withdrawal procedures
