# Curated Sponsorship Policy

## Purpose

Father's Business Bible Study may eventually accept a small number of direct sponsorships from carefully reviewed businesses or ministries. Sponsorship must help fund dependable Bible study work without turning Scripture, study, prayer, preaching, or teaching into advertising inventory.

This policy prepares a limited pilot. It does not authorize a sponsor, publish an advertisement, collect payment, or imply an endorsement.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- No third-party advertising network, behavioral profile, tracking pixel, cross-site targeting, or sale of user data.
- No sponsor placement inside KJV Bible text, Word Lens, Strong's, Webster 1828, cross-references, commentary, prayer, journal, sermon preparation, lesson preparation, presentations, or children's material.
- No interruption of reading, audio, video, teaching, or worship with an advertisement.
- No sponsor may influence doctrinal review, resource ranking, search results, editorial conclusions, public-domain decisions, or permission decisions.
- No sponsor receives user email addresses, prayer entries, notes, sermons, lessons, searches, reading history, or account data.
- Every placement must say `Sponsor` or `Ministry sponsor` plainly. Paid placement must never look like an editorial recommendation.

## Eligible Placement

The first pilot may use no more than one sponsor block on any eligible page and no more than three active sponsors across the public site.

Eligible public pages:

- coming-soon and launch updates
- support and stewardship
- partnership information
- an optional public sponsor acknowledgment page
- a library footer after all study content, only after separate review

Ineligible surfaces include the Bible reader, study workspace, library reader, commentary explorer, sermon or lesson builder, presentation controller, projector view, prayer, journal, radio listening, search results, and account areas.

## Sponsor Review

Every proposed sponsor receives a manual review covering:

1. ownership, leadership, public contact, and operating history
2. product, service, ministry purpose, and claims
3. doctrinal fit for a ministry sponsor
4. privacy practices and landing-page behavior
5. advertising copy, image, destination, and audience
6. complaints, refunds, safety concerns, and conflicts of interest

The project may refuse or remove a sponsor at any time when the relationship could harm users, compromise the mission, or create confusion about endorsement.

## Prohibited Sponsors And Claims

Do not accept sponsorship from or for:

- pornography, sexual services, occult practice, gambling, alcohol, recreational drugs, or tobacco
- political candidates, parties, campaigns, or lobbying organizations
- predatory lending, speculative financial promotions, deceptive investment claims, or multi-level marketing recruitment
- miracle cures, guaranteed health results, manipulative fundraising, or unverifiable testimonies
- businesses built on surveillance advertising, data brokerage, deceptive subscriptions, or dark patterns
- ministries or resources that contradict the project's doctrinal basis or misrepresent their beliefs
- products that exploit children, vulnerable adults, churches, missionaries, or people in financial distress

## Commercial Structure

- Begin with fixed 90-day direct agreements, not auctioned impressions.
- Publish no rate card until audience size and operating costs are measured honestly.
- Separate sponsorship accounting from gifts, donations, affiliate revenue, book royalties, and marketplace sales.
- Record exact term, placement, creative, destination, price, invoice, refund terms, removal rights, and renewal choice.
- Do not auto-renew the first pilot.
- Use privacy-preserving aggregate click counts only if measurement is necessary; do not create user-level sponsor profiles.

## Endorsement And Ministry Labels

A business sponsorship funds the work but is not a recommendation of every product, statement, or practice of the business.

A ministry sponsor requires closer doctrinal review. Even then, the label means the ministry supports the project; it does not mean the project or ministry endorses every resource, speaker, historical work, or conclusion appearing elsewhere.

## Pilot Gate

Before accepting the first payment:

- counsel reviews the agreement, disclosures, privacy language, and tax treatment
- accounting and reconciliation procedures are tested
- the sponsor-interest form and internal approval log are working
- a named person owns complaint handling and takedown decisions
- the placement is tested on mobile and desktop
- the sponsor sees and approves the exact final placement

Until those gates pass, the public site may collect interest but must state that no advertising inventory is currently for sale.
