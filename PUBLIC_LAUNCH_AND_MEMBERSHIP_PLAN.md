# Public Launch And Membership Plan

Use this plan to move Father's Business Bible Study from private beta toward public use without confusing free resources, licensed resources, admin-only material, and paid app features.

## Launch Posture

The app is close to a public beta, but not a full commercial library platform yet.

Public beta should emphasize:

- KJV Bible reading and study
- Library discovery for verified public-domain resources
- Commentary Explorer and Study Workspace
- Strong's, Webster, TSK, dictionaries, maps, timelines, and background tools
- Prayer, journal, notes, reading progress, and study sessions
- Sermon preparation and simple presentation export
- Feedback and resource issue reporting

Keep public beta modest, honest, and useful. Do not promise complete Logos-style coverage yet.

## What Can Be Free

These should remain free or broadly accessible:

- KJV Bible reading
- Public-domain library resources
- Permission-cleared free resources where the rights holder requires free access
- Thru the Bible / J. Vernon McGee resources that grant free reproduction or linking only
- Basic search
- Basic commentary reading
- Basic Bible tools
- Basic sermon and lesson notes
- Feedback and issue reporting

Do not put a resource behind a paywall if its permission terms say copied material must remain free or cannot be sold.

## What Membership Can Charge For

Paid membership should charge for app value, not for restricted free material.

Possible paid features:

- Cloud sync across devices
- Saved study sessions
- Advanced notes and journal organization
- Advanced sermon workspace
- Presentation remote control and church-use polish
- Export history and sermon archive
- Personal private uploads
- Larger storage quotas
- Advanced audio playlists
- Follow-along reading workflows
- Advanced search across owned notes and public-domain library content
- Original premium study packs created by Father's Business Mastery Resources
- Licensed resources only when a written license allows paid access

## Rights Separation

Every resource should be one of these:

- Public Domain
- Permission Granted
- Permission Needed
- Personal Use Only
- Do Not Import
- Admin Review Only

Never mix these categories in public screens without clear labels.

Rules:

- Public-domain resources may be indexed, searched, and displayed after source review.
- Permission-granted resources may only be used within the exact granted scope.
- Metadata-only permission means title, description, graphics, and links only.
- Link/excerpt permission does not mean full-text permission.
- Audio/video permission is separate from text permission.
- TTS/AI narration permission is separate from ebook permission.
- Paid subscription permission is separate from free beta permission.
- No response does not equal permission.

## Founding Ministry Partner Path

When a ministry grants permission, add it slowly.

1. Save the permission email or PDF.
2. Update the permission tracker.
3. Create an admin-only ministry record.
4. Add public listing only after public mention is allowed.
5. Add resource cards using the permitted scope.
6. Link users back to the official ministry site.
7. Add excerpts only if the scope allows excerpts.
8. Keep full books, audio, and video blocked unless explicitly granted.

Northstar Ministries / Dr. David H. Sorenson is a good first model because permission includes links, excerpts, and cover graphics, but title-level review is still required.

## Paid Launch Readiness

Do not launch paid membership until these are true:

- Sign-in works reliably.
- Admin-only areas are hidden from normal users.
- Library, Bible, Search, Prayer, Journal, Sermons, and Presentations are stable on mobile.
- Resource rights labels are clear.
- Payment terms explain what is being purchased.
- Free resources remain accessible according to their terms.
- Refund and support contact path exists.
- Feedback and bug reports are easy.
- Privacy policy and terms are drafted.
- Supabase auth, database, and RLS are reviewed.
- Backups/export paths are tested.

## Suggested Launch Path

### Step 1: Private Beta

Invite 3-5 trusted testers.

Focus:

- Reading
- Search
- Study Workspace
- Library discovery
- Prayer and Journal
- Sermon prep
- iPad preaching mode
- Presentation export

### Step 2: Public Free Beta

Open to a small public audience.

Focus:

- Clear free library
- Feedback
- Stability
- No paid promises yet

### Step 3: Support / Donate

Add an optional donate button before paid membership.

This lets people help without forcing a subscription before the platform is mature.

### Step 4: Membership

Add membership after users are already receiving value.

Charge for:

- sync
- workflow depth
- storage
- private uploads
- advanced sermon/presentation tools
- original premium study packs
- properly licensed premium resources

## Near-Term Priorities

1. Keep filling commentary, Strong's, TSK, Webster, dictionary, map, and timeline coverage.
2. Finish iPad preaching and presentation QA.
3. Add more public-domain audio pilots through R2.
4. Add ministry partner pages only where permission is clear.
5. Build a simple landing page and public beta invitation.
6. Prepare pricing later, after usage feedback.

## Current Recommendation

For now, launch free public beta first, with an optional donate button. Add paid membership after testers prove which features they would actually use weekly.
