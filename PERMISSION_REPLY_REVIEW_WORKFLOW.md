# Permission Reply Review Workflow

Use this workflow when an author, ministry, pastor, or publisher replies to a permission request.

## Core Rule

A friendly reply is not broad permission.

Do not mark any resource as approved until the reply clearly states the exact resource, exact permitted uses, attribution terms, and whether the permission applies to free beta, free public access, paid access, audio, video, excerpts, or full text.

## Reply Categories

| Reply type | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No response yet | Nothing has been granted. | Keep as Permission Requested or Needs Follow-up. |
| Redirected | They gave another contact or form. | Record the contact and send a new request only after review. |
| Metadata/linking allowed | App may list title, author, description, and official link. | Mark metadata permission only. Do not import text. |
| Excerpt allowed | A limited quote/sample may be used. | Record exact length, title, attribution, and display limits. |
| Private beta allowed | Use is limited to private beta testers. | Keep hidden from public users and record beta limits. |
| Public app allowed | Resource may appear to normal users. | Confirm whether free-only or paid access is allowed. |
| Full digital text allowed | Full text may appear in reader/search. | Record title, edition, attribution, term, and removal rules. |
| Audio/video embed allowed | Official embedded media is allowed. | Use official embed/link only unless hosting is separately approved. |
| Audio/video hosting allowed | App may store/stream media. | Record storage, bandwidth, attribution, and term limits. |
| TTS or narration allowed | App may generate or provide audio narration. | Record whether browser TTS, AI TTS, and human narration are distinct rights. |
| Denied | Permission was refused. | Mark denied; do not import. |
| Unclear | The reply does not answer exact rights. | Ask clarification; do not import. |

## Before Updating Public Library

Confirm all items below:

- exact title
- exact author/ministry/publisher
- exact edition or source URL
- permission signer name and role
- permitted use
- denied use
- attribution wording
- free beta permission
- free public permission
- paid access permission
- excerpt permission
- full text permission
- audio/video permission
- TTS/narration permission
- expiration or revocation terms
- removal request process

## Safety Labels

Use these labels in the tracker:

- `No Response Yet`
- `Needs Clarification`
- `Metadata/Link Only`
- `Excerpt Permission Only`
- `Private Beta Only`
- `Approved - Limited`
- `Approved - Public`
- `Denied`
- `Do Not Import`

## What To Save

Save or record:

- original email date
- reply date
- sender name and email
- organization
- full reply text or stored evidence path
- exact resource discussed
- final decision
- next follow-up date

Do not rely on memory. If it is not written down, treat it as not approved.
