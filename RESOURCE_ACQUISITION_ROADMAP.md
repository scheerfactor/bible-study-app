# Resource Acquisition Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap expands the permissions database into a practical acquisition plan. The goal is not to collect every possible Christian book. The goal is to build a curated, KJV-friendly, Baptist-friendly, dispensational-friendly library that helps users study Scripture clearly.

## Foundation Documents

Use these documents together:

- `DOCTRINAL_BASIS.md` states the doctrinal reference point for resource review.
- `RESOURCE_REVIEW.md` defines the review board workflow and scoring rubric.
- `AUTHOR_PROFILE.md` provides the template for author and ministry pages.
- `RESOURCE_VETTING_GUIDELINES.md` defines resource labels, caution rules, and review criteria.
- `STUDY_WORKSPACE_ROADMAP.md` describes the long-term passage-level study environment.

## Guiding Principle

Do not label authors broadly as "safe" or "unsafe." Classify each resource according to how it fits the app's doctrinal basis, audience, rights status, and use case.

Recommended public-facing collection labels:

- Core Library
- Recommended
- Historical Collection
- Comparative Study
- Needs Review
- Do Not Include

## Tier 1: Highest Priority

These authors and ministries align strongly with the target audience and should be pursued first, one resource at a time.

Independent Baptist and ministry-aligned targets:

- David Cloud / Way of Life Literature
- Mark Cahill
- Dr. S. M. Davis
- Dr. Bo Wagner
- Dr. William P. Grady
- Dr. David H. Sorenson
- John Goetsch
- Douglas Hammett
- Bogard Press
- Ronald T. Meeks
- Sword of the Lord selected resources

Recommended first ask:

- metadata and official links
- one approved excerpt
- one sample chapter or article
- one sermon, lesson, or media item
- future licensing conversation only after trust is established

## Tier 2: Classic Dispensational Resources

These can provide outstanding verse-by-verse and doctrinal material when source, edition, and rights are verified.

- H. A. Ironside
- Lewis Sperry Chafer
- C. I. Scofield
- Clarence Larkin
- Arno C. Gaebelein
- William Kelly
- C. H. Mackintosh
- Andrew Bonar
- G. Campbell Morgan
- F. B. Meyer

Recommended first action:

- verify public-domain editions
- separate text rights from chart/image rights
- check OCR quality
- group volumes and editions cleanly
- avoid modern edited editions unless permission is documented

## Tier 3: Classic Preaching

These authors can strengthen sermons, devotional reading, illustrations, and preaching preparation.

- Charles Spurgeon
- Alexander Maclaren
- Vance Havner
- Oliver B. Greene
- John R. Rice
- Leonard Ravenhill
- J. Vernon McGee

Recommended first action:

- import verified public-domain works where available
- request permission for modern copyrighted works
- keep sermon/audio/transcript rights separate
- label doctrinal perspective where needed

## Tier 4: Study Tools

Reference works should be integrated directly into Bible study.

Priority tools:

- Willmington's Bible Handbook
- Willmington's Guide to the Bible
- Treasury of Scripture Knowledge
- Webster 1828 Dictionary
- Nave's Topical Bible
- Easton's Bible Dictionary
- Smith's Bible Dictionary
- Hitchcock's Bible Names
- Torrey New Topical Textbook

Integration goals:

- Bible Reader
- Passage Guide
- Word Connection Mode
- Study Workspace
- Sermon Builder
- Teaching notes

## Audio

Audio should eventually become a major strength of the app.

Priority targets:

- Thru the Bible
- Way of Life
- S. M. Davis
- Mark Cahill
- Faith Baptist ministries that publish sermons
- Independent Baptist conferences with permission

Possible uses:

- official links
- official embeds
- sermon metadata
- transcript excerpts
- app-hosted audio only with permission

Do not download, rehost, transcribe, summarize, or generate audio from copyrighted material unless permission is documented.

## Video

Video can help the app become a teaching platform, but rights must be handled carefully.

Priority targets:

- Mark Cahill
- S. M. Davis
- Bo Wagner
- David Cloud
- John Goetsch

Possible uses:

- official links
- official embeds
- teaching series pages
- sermon/lesson companion media

## Long-Term Study Experience

The strongest version of the app connects everything at the verse and passage level.

Example for John 3:16:

- KJV text
- commentaries
- audio
- video
- maps
- dictionary entries
- TSK cross references
- Strong's word studies
- user highlights
- journal notes
- sermon outline

Everything for that verse should be reachable from one place.

## Added Review Fields

The acquisition CSV should track:

- primary audience
- verse-by-verse
- topical
- audio available
- video available
- public API/RSS
- mobile friendly
- embed allowed
- AI summary allowed
- license obtained
- expiration date

These fields are now represented in `data/library/acquisition/permission-targets.csv`.
