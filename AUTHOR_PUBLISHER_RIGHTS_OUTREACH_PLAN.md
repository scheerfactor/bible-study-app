# Author and Publisher Rights Outreach Plan

## Purpose

Prepare Father's Business Bible Study to request permission for modern copyrighted books, commentaries, study notes, sermons, audio, and excerpts without risking public distribution of unapproved content.

## Readiness Status

The app is close enough to begin conversations with rights holders because it can show:

- a working Bible-centered study platform
- a curated public-domain library
- admin-only rights tracking
- public/private resource separation
- no automatic public publishing of unreviewed imports
- a clear mission for pastors, Sunday School teachers, missionaries, and serious Bible students

Do not promise paid placement or broad public distribution until written permission terms are documented.

## What To Ask For First

Start with narrow, easier permissions:

1. Permission to list metadata and link to the publisher/ministry store.
2. Permission to show short excerpts or sample chapters.
3. Permission to include selected books in a private beta.
4. Permission to include full digital text in the app.
5. Permission to create text-to-speech or audiobook access.
6. Permission to include content in a paid subscription later.

Do not ask for everything in the first email unless the rights holder already has a licensing process.

## First Email Template

Subject: Permission question for a Bible-centered study app

Hello,

My name is Stephen Scheer. I am building Father's Business Bible Study, a Bible-centered web app for Christians, Sunday School teachers, pastors, missionaries, evangelists, and serious Bible students.

The app currently includes public-domain resources only unless permission is documented. I would like to ask about permission for selected resources from [AUTHOR / MINISTRY / PUBLISHER] to be represented in the app.

The first request is limited:

- list the title, author, description, and cover/metadata if allowed
- link users back to your official store or ministry page
- include a short excerpt or sample if you approve
- discuss whether full digital access, audiobook/TTS access, or paid subscription inclusion could be licensed later

Nothing would be published publicly without written approval. If you have a permissions form, licensing contact, or preferred process, please send me the right next step.

## Follow-Up Schedule

- Day 0: Send first request.
- Day 7: Follow up once with a shorter note.
- Day 21: Mark "Needs follow-up" or "No response yet."
- Day 45: Move to "Permission Needed" and do not import.
- Any approval: store the exact written terms, contact, date, permitted uses, and restrictions.

## Permission Fields To Track

- public app permission
- private-user import permission
- excerpt permission
- audiobook permission
- AI/TTS narration permission
- paid subscription permission
- free beta permission
- royalty terms
- term length
- territory
- revocation/removal requirements

## Safety Rules

- Owning a physical book does not grant public digital distribution rights.
- No response does not equal permission.
- Public-domain status must be verified by edition, not just author death year.
- Modern revised editions of public-domain works can still be copyrighted.
- Audio rights are separate from text rights.
- AI/TTS narration rights should be requested separately.
- User uploads remain private unless the platform has publication permission.

## What To Say In A Call

"We are not trying to scrape or republish content without permission. The app is built around Scripture first, and copyrighted books stay blocked unless permission is documented. We can start with linking and excerpts, then discuss licensing if the fit is good."

## What Not To Do Yet

- Do not ask for entire publisher catalogs.
- Do not promise revenue share terms before traffic data exists.
- Do not upload copyrighted PDFs as public resources.
- Do not let personal-use files become public.
- Do not use paid audio/TTS for copyrighted text unless narration rights are explicit.
