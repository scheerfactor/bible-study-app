## Project Description

Father's Business Bible Study is a Scripture-first Bible study app for Christians, Sunday School teachers, pastors, missionaries, evangelists, and serious Bible students. It helps users read Scripture, study Scripture, use public-domain commentaries and Bible tools, journal and pray from Scripture, prepare lessons and sermons, and organize Christian library reading.

## Rights Safety Statement

Father's Business Bible Study does not publicly publish copyrighted books, commentaries, sermons, audio, video, scans, or excerpts unless permission is documented. Public resources are limited to public-domain or permission-cleared material. Unapproved resources remain hidden from normal users.

## Narrow First Request

Use this wording in permission forms:

```text
I am requesting permission to list [TITLE] by [AUTHOR] inside Father's Business Bible Study with basic metadata, a short description, and an official purchase/ministry link. If allowed, I would also like permission to include a short approved excerpt or sample chapter.

This request does not assume permission to publish the full text. I am asking first for the correct permission process and whether metadata/linking and a short approved excerpt are permitted.

Future full digital text, private beta inclusion, audiobook/TTS access, or paid subscription inclusion would be discussed separately and would not be used without written approval.
```

## Uses To Request

For the first form submission, choose only the uses that match the form:

- metadata and official link
- short approved excerpt
- private beta inclusion if available
- future full digital licensing inquiry

Do not request audio, video, AI narration, or full catalog rights unless the form specifically asks about them.

## Before Submitting Any Form

- Pick one exact title.
- Confirm the edition and publisher.
- Confirm the resource is not already public domain.
- Keep the request narrow.
- Save screenshots or confirmation numbers.
- Record the submission date in `data/library/acquisition/permission-email-send-log.csv` or a dedicated form log.
