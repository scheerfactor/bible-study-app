## Agreement Checklist For Any Positive Reply

Do not treat a positive reply as complete until the agreement identifies:

| Term | Required detail |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Exact title, edition, format, and supplied files |
| Rights holder | Legal person or entity authorized to grant the rights |
| Permitted use | Hosting, reading, search, passage indexing, samples, sales, streaming, downloads, or other exact uses |
| Audience | Public samples, authenticated purchasers, institutions, classrooms, or other named users |
| Purchase route | Publisher checkout with entitlement or authorized in-app sale |
| Economics | Retail price, revenue share or royalty, taxes, refunds, payment timing, and reporting |
| Territory and term | Countries, start date, duration, renewal, and exclusivity or non-exclusivity |
| Updates | Corrections, replacement files, new editions, and who supplies them |
| Security | Authentication, file protection, download rules, account sharing, and breach response |
| Attribution | Required credit, copyright notice, cover, description, and links |
| Withdrawal | Notice period, removal process, and treatment of samples and metadata |
| Existing purchasers | Whether access continues after withdrawal or the pilot ends |
| Reserved rights | Audio, video, transcript, AI narration, TTS, translation, print, and classroom rights not expressly granted |
