# Doctrinal Basis

## Purpose

Father's Business Bible Study needs a clear doctrinal basis so resources can be reviewed consistently. This document is the reference point for library curation, commentary recommendations, sermon material, audio/video permissions, and future publisher conversations.

This document does not replace Scripture. Scripture is the final authority. The purpose here is to state the app's working doctrinal position so reviewers can compare resources against a known standard rather than personal preference.

## Scripture

The Bible is the inspired, preserved, authoritative Word of God and is the final rule for faith and practice.

For this app:

- Scripture quotations and primary reading use the King James Bible.
- Study tools should support, not correct or undermine, the Bible text.
- Resources that attack confidence in the King James Bible should not be core recommendations.
- Textual criticism, Bible history, and translation discussions may be included only with clear labels and review notes.

## The Trinity

There is one true and living God, eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. These three are one God, equal in divine nature, power, and glory.

## The Person Of Christ

The Lord Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died as the substitutionary sacrifice for sinners, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the right hand of the Father, and will return.

## Salvation By Grace Through Faith

Salvation is by grace through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, not by works, sacraments, church membership, baptism, or personal merit.

The Gospel should be clear:

- man is sinful and guilty before God
- Christ died for our sins
- Christ was buried
- Christ rose again
- salvation is received by faith
- assurance rests in Christ and the promise of God

Resources that confuse the Gospel with works, sacramentalism, or religious performance must not be recommended as core teaching resources.

## Eternal Security

The app's working position is that the believer is kept by the power of God and has eternal life in Christ. Resources that differ on assurance or security should be reviewed carefully and labeled where needed.

## The Church

The local church is central to Christian life, worship, discipleship, preaching, missions, baptism, the Lord's Supper, and service. The app is Baptist-friendly and should especially help pastors, Sunday School teachers, church members, missionaries, evangelists, and serious Bible students.

## Baptism

Believer's baptism by immersion is the app's working doctrinal position. Baptism is an act of obedience and public identification with Christ, not a means of regeneration or saving grace.

Resources that teach baptismal regeneration or sacramental salvation should not be core recommendations.

## The Lord's Supper

The Lord's Supper is a memorial ordinance for believers, pointing back to the death of Christ and forward to His return. It is not a saving sacrament and does not confer grace by the elements themselves.

## Israel And The Church

The app is dispensational-friendly and keeps Israel and the Church distinct in God's program. Resources with covenant theology, replacement theology, or strongly allegorical prophecy interpretation may still have historical or comparative value, but should be labeled clearly and should not be default recommendations for new believers.

## Future Events

The app is friendly to a literal, premillennial, dispensational understanding of prophecy. Future-events resources must be reviewed carefully because prophecy material can easily become speculative.

Recommended prophecy resources should:

- keep Scripture primary
- distinguish clear doctrine from inference
- avoid date-setting
- avoid sensationalism
- handle Israel, the Church, the Tribulation, the Second Coming, and the Kingdom carefully

## Satan

Satan is a real personal being, the adversary of God and man. Resources should treat spiritual warfare biblically and soberly, avoiding superstition, sensationalism, or charismatic excess.

## Angels

Angels are real created beings who serve God. Demonology and angelology resources should be reviewed carefully and should not be allowed to distract from Scripture, Christ, and practical obedience.

## Creation

God created the heavens and the earth. The app should be friendly to a conservative creation position and should review evolution-related and apologetics resources carefully.

Resources on creation, science, and apologetics should be evaluated for:

- faithfulness to Scripture
- clarity
- usefulness for families, teens, teachers, and evangelists
- avoidance of needless speculation

## Human Nature

Man was created by God, in the image of God, and is accountable to God. Because of sin, all people need salvation through Jesus Christ.

Resources on sin, temptation, addiction, family, marriage, purity, anger, alcohol, and Christian living should be practical, biblical, and Gospel-clear.

## Christian Living

The Christian life should be marked by faith, prayer, holiness, obedience, love, service, evangelism, missions, local church faithfulness, and growth in Scripture.

Resources should encourage:

- Bible reading and study
- prayer
- personal holiness
- church faithfulness
- family responsibility
- evangelism
- missions
- humility and charity

## Resource Review Use

Every resource should be compared against this doctrinal basis before being marked as:

- Core Library
- Recommended
- Historical Collection
- Comparative Study
- Needs Review
- Do Not Include

Differences on secondary issues do not automatically disqualify a resource. But users should be told what kind of resource they are reading, why it is included, and where caution is needed.
