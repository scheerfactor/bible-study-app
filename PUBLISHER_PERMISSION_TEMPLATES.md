# Publisher and Author Permission Templates

## Use These Rules First

- Ask for one title or one small set first.
- Ask for written permission.
- Store the exact permission terms in the Rights Management Center.
- Do not publish copyrighted text, audio, video, or scans until permission is approved.
- Ask about audio/TTS separately from ebook/text rights.
- No response does not equal permission.

## Template 1: Full Digital Library Permission

Subject: Permission request for [TITLE] in Father's Business Bible Study

Hello [NAME],

My name is Stephen Scheer. I am building Father's Business Bible Study, a Scripture-first Bible study app for Christians, Sunday School teachers, pastors, missionaries, evangelists, and serious Bible students.

I would like to request permission to include [TITLE] by [AUTHOR] in the app's digital Library.

The app is rights-first. Public resources are limited to public-domain or permission-cleared materials, and unapproved resources remain hidden from normal users.

Requested use:

- display the full text inside the app
- allow users to search the title
- allow reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, and notes
- recommend the book in related Bible study workflows
- include source, author, publisher, and rights metadata

If this is possible, please let me know the permission process, licensing terms, restrictions, attribution requirements, and whether the permission applies to free beta access, paid access, or both.

Thank you,

Stephen Scheer
