# Library Expansion Plan

Father's Business Bible Study should grow the Library carefully: public-domain first, rights documented, doctrinal review visible, and resources curated instead of dumped into the app.

## Next Recommended Additions

### H. A. Ironside Books
- Priority: high
- Use: expository helps, devotional reading, teaching preparation
- Review need: confirm edition/source rights before import
- Notes: keep as a second commentary/resource voice; do not let commentary dominate Scripture

### Spurgeon Books And Sermons
- Priority: high
- Use: preaching/devotional, illustrations, pastoral warmth
- Review need: import verified public-domain sermons/books only
- Notes: label as devotional/practical; preserve source notices where required

### E. M. Bounds
- Priority: high
- Use: prayer, ministry burden, devotional reading
- Review need: verify source text and edition
- Notes: strong fit for the Prayer shelf and listening workflow

### J. C. Ryle
- Priority: high
- Use: Christian living, Gospel clarity, practical holiness
- Review need: verify public-domain works and source metadata
- Notes: label Anglican background where useful; recommended with discernment on secondary differences

### D. L. Moody
- Priority: medium
- Use: evangelism, Christian life, practical ministry
- Review need: verify edition and rights metadata
- Notes: good for new believers and outreach-focused readers

### Baptist History
- Priority: high
- Use: Baptist distinctives, church history, historical context
- Review need: doctrinal review before import
- Notes: include perspective notes; do not import polemical material without review

### KJV-Related Books
- Priority: medium
- Use: textual issues, Bible preservation, KJV defense
- Review need: careful doctrinal and source review
- Notes: avoid uncertain rights, inflammatory framing, or poor-quality scans

### Prayer Books
- Priority: high
- Use: prayer life, church prayer meetings, pastoral encouragement
- Review need: rights and doctrinal review
- Notes: best candidates for listen mode and short reading sessions

### Bible Study Books
- Priority: high
- Use: introductions, surveys, handbooks, dictionaries, topical helps
- Review need: avoid modern copyrighted handbooks unless licensed
- Notes: prefer searchable metadata and chapter recommendations before full-text import

### Preaching And Teaching Books
- Priority: medium
- Use: Sunday school preparation, sermon structure, illustrations
- Review need: rights and doctrinal review
- Notes: keep presentation software out of scope until teaching notes/export is mature

### Missionary Biographies
- Priority: medium
- Use: missions, prayer, examples of faithfulness
- Review need: source rights and historical/editorial review
- Notes: label historical value and doctrinal perspective when needed

## Import Rules

- Import only resources with documented source URL, rights status, public-domain status, and commercial-use notes.
- Keep required license or Project Gutenberg notices where applicable.
- Do not import resources marked `needs review` or `do not import`.
- Do not import false religion, cult literature, or resources rejected by doctrinal review.
- Add labels such as `Devotional classic`, `Historical value`, `Baptist history`, `Use with discernment`, and `Not all doctrine endorsed` where appropriate.
- Add metadata first, then small sample text, then full text only after validation.

## App Growth Order

1. Metadata and rights review.
2. Searchable Library listing.
3. Reader quality and listen controls.
4. Chapter recommendations.
5. Teaching export connections.
6. Future storage move for large files and audio.
