# Father's Business Bible Study Platform

## Long-Term Vision

### Mission

Create the most useful Bible-centered platform for Christians, Bible students, Sunday School teachers, pastors, missionaries, evangelists, and serious readers.

The goal is not to build the biggest library.

The goal is not to build the most complicated software.

The goal is to help Christians:

- Read Scripture
- Study Scripture
- Understand Scripture
- Memorize Scripture
- Pray Scripture
- Journal Scripture
- Teach Scripture
- Preach Scripture
- Present Scripture

from one connected platform.

## Core Philosophy

Scripture first.

Everything else supports Scripture.

Resources, commentaries, dictionaries, Strong's, books, illustrations, quotes, and presentations exist to help users understand and communicate God's Word.

## Resource Policy

Scripture remains authoritative.

Commentaries are helpful but secondary.

Resources may include authors with doctrinal differences.

Use labels such as:

- Recommended
- Use with Discernment
- Historical Value
- Not All Doctrine Endorsed

Do not alter original text.

Provide guidance.
