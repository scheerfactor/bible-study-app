# Founding Beta Runway

Working target: Thursday, April 15, 2027 at 7:00 PM Eastern

This is a prayerful target for a founding free public beta. It is not a promise to ship an unfinished product, and it is not a promise that every future library, media, radio, presentation, or storefront idea will be present on day one.

## Launch Floor

The founding beta opens only when a new user can reliably:

1. Read and navigate the KJV.
2. Define a word with reviewed Webster 1828 and verified Strong's information.
3. Follow reviewed cross-references and passage-linked commentary.
4. Save work locally, restore it, and export it.
5. Turn one passage into a useful note, lesson, sermon outline, or presentation draft.
6. Report a problem or correction to a monitored queue.

The public Library must contain only public-domain, permission-cleared, or official-link resources with visible source and rights boundaries.

## What Is Not Promised For Day One

- every public-domain Christian book
- modern copyrighted books without written permission
- hosted sermon, audiobook, video, podcast, or radio catalogs without documented rights
- a paid subscription or book marketplace before support and accounting are ready
- replacement of Logos, e-Sword, SwordSearcher, Olive Tree, or every tool a serious student already uses

The founding beta should be valuable as a focused companion even when a user keeps other Bible software.

## Runway

### August-September 2026: Stabilize The Floor

- close public/private access gaps
- verify feedback delivery and correction handling
- finish real-device timing for Bible loading and Word Lens
- test backup, restore, export, and signed-in non-admin access
- identify 5-10 trusted testers

### October-November 2026: Prove The Workflow

- record unedited internal Hosea 4 and John 3 study sessions
- fix every confusing or broken step found in those recordings
- run the same workflow on phone, tablet, and desktop
- collect tester completion time and week-one return evidence

### December 2026-January 2027: Build Trust And Partnerships

- publish privacy, terms, support, copyright, and correction pages
- approach a small number of trusted authors, ministries, and publishers
- begin with official listings and purchase links
- record item-level permission for text, covers, excerpts, audio, video, narration, sales, and paid access separately
- prepare one or two partnership pilots without announcing unsigned titles

### February 2027: Release Candidate

- freeze founding-beta scope
- test one full backup and restore
- rerun library, commentary, Webster, Strong's, TSK, media, lint, build, and public-access gates
- resolve critical tester failures before adding features

### March 2027: Honest Public Preview

- publish the short teaser and complete Study To Teach demonstration
- show actual loading time and actual working screens
- open a no-payment interest list
- publish known limitations and the exact founding-beta scope

### April 2027: Final Gate

- admit the first users in small waves only if every launch-floor gate passes
- keep access free during the founding beta
- delay the opening if privacy, data safety, rights, support, or core study reliability remains uncertain

## Partnership Boundary

A useful partnership can begin without hosting a copyrighted book. The safe sequence is:

1. Official author/resource profile and purchase link.
2. Written permission for specific metadata, cover art, and excerpts.
3. Written permission for hosted text or media, including territories and commercial use.
4. Author review of attribution and presentation.
5. Written sales terms, reporting, refunds, and payment schedule before any in-app sale.

No response is not permission. Owning a book is not permission. A free resource is not automatically licensed for redistribution or inclusion in a paid product.

## Decision Rule

Protect trust before the date. If the launch floor is ready, open a small founding beta. If it is not ready, keep the preview available, explain the remaining gate plainly, and move the opening without pretending otherwise.
