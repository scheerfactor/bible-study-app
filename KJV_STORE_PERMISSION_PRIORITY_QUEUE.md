# KJV Store Permission Priority Queue

This queue turns The KJV Store discovery review into a practical next-step list. It does **not** approve any title, author, publisher, cover image, description, audio, video, or excerpt for public use.

Source reviewed: https://www.thekjvstore.com/christian-books/

## Rules

- Do not send mass emails automatically.
- Do not add broad publishers to the main permission tracker until Stephen reviews the exact target.
- Do not import retail product text, cover images, descriptions, commentary notes, Bible notes, or media.
- Use this queue to decide which ministries or publishers deserve a carefully reviewed permission request.
- Prefer public-domain equivalents first when they meet the same user need.

## Permission Questions To Ask

- May the app list selected titles with official purchase links?
- May approved cover images or promotional graphics be displayed?
- May short approved excerpts be displayed?
- May audio or video be embedded if hosted by the rights holder?
- May purchased or user-owned resources be tracked privately without public distribution?
- Who is the correct permissions contact?

## Do Not Ask Yet

Do not ask for AI training, AI summaries, full catalog rights, offline redistribution, or broad digital reproduction in a first email unless the rights holder raises those topics. Start with limited listing, linking, and selected excerpt permission.
