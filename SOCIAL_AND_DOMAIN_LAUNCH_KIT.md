# Social, Website, Domain, and Video Launch Kit

## Purpose

Prepare Father's Business Bible Study for public visibility without rushing into a broad paid launch.

The app should first be presented as:

> a Scripture-first Bible study, library, sermon preparation, prayer, journal, and presentation app in active beta.

Avoid calling it finished. Avoid promising a replacement for every major Bible software platform. The best early message is simple:

> Read, study, pray, journal, and prepare Bible lessons from one clean KJV-focused workspace.

## Current Public Readiness

The app is close enough for a public-facing page, demo videos, and permission conversations.

It is not yet ready for a large paid public launch.

Recommended order:

1. Private beta with trusted testers.
2. Public landing page and waitlist.
3. Facebook page and YouTube channel.
4. Free public beta with donation/support option.
5. Paid membership only after sync, support, admin protection, payments, and licensing rules are stable.

## Logo Direction

Keep the logo simple and serious.

Good visual directions:

- open Bible
- simple cross
- lamp/light
- pulpit and Bible
- book + path

Avoid:

- cluttered seals
- tiny theological symbols that do not read well on mobile
- overly ornate church art
- anything that makes the app look like clip art
