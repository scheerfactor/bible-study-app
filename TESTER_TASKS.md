# Tester Tasks

Use this checklist for each beta testing session.

## Phone Test

- Open the app on a phone-sized screen.
- Confirm Today loads and does not feel crowded.
- Tap Bible and go to John 3.
- Open Passage Guide.
- Open Commentary.
- Start Bible listening, pause, resume, and stop.
- Check that the bottom navigation does not cover important controls.

## Study Depth Test

- Check John 3, Romans 8, Amos 1, Daniel 7, and Revelation 13.
- Confirm each chapter shows Bible text, study access, commentary access, and background/study helps where available.
- Tap words such as believe, born, spirit, adoption, transgression, beast, mark, vision, and Zion.
- Confirm missing definitions or Strong's entries fail gracefully.

## Library Test

- Open Library.
- Search for Spurgeon, Moody, Ryle, prayer, missions, and commentary.
- Open one book.
- Test Read, Listen, Stop, progress, and Continue Reading.
- Confirm duplicate-looking resources are not confusing.

## Prayer And Journal Test

- Create a prayer request.
- Mark it prayed.
- Create a journal entry from a Bible verse.
- Export prayer or journal data if available.
- Refresh and confirm local data remains.

## Sermon And Presentation Test

- Create or open a John 3 sermon/lesson.
- Add a verse to the sermon from Bible or Passage Guide.
- Generate or open slides.
- Preview and enter present mode.
- Export sermon notes or slide outline if available.

## Settings And Feedback Test

- Check sync/status wording.
- Export user data.
- Submit beta feedback with the page or passage being tested.

## Pass Criteria

- No blank screens.
- No console error overlay.
- No horizontal mobile overflow.
- Stop audio works without refresh.
- Core local data persists after refresh.
- Unfinished areas are clearly marked beta/coming soon.
