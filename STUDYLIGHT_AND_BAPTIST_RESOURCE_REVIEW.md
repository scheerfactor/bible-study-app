## Baptist Priority Authors

Priority additions, public-domain/rights-safe only:

- John Gill: original Exposition and Body of Divinity sources, not modernized copyrighted adaptations.
- Alexander Maclaren: Expositions of Holy Scripture and sermon materials.
- F. B. Meyer: Through the Bible, biographical/devotional/commentary works.
- B. H. Carroll: Interpretation of the English Bible and Baptist teaching works.
- A. B. Earle: evangelism, revival, soul-winning, and practical Baptist ministry works.
- Thomas Armitage, John T. Christian, David Benedict, Isaac Backus: Baptist history.
- J. M. Pendleton, John Broadus, E. C. Dargan: Baptist theology, preaching, and church order where editions are public domain.

## Source Strategy

Preferred sources:

1. Project Gutenberg for plain text public-domain books.
2. Internet Archive for original scans plus OCR, with OCR quality labels.
3. HathiTrust Public Domain / Google Books Full View for edition verification.
4. CCEL for public-domain Christian classics where redistribution is allowed.
5. StudyLight for discovery and comparison, not as the default text source.
6. BibleSupport only when license/permission terms are documented clearly.

## Safety Notes

- Free online access is not the same as public redistribution permission.
- Modernized/adapted editions can create new rights even when the original author is public domain.
- StudyLight is excellent for discovery, but imports should use source files with explicit public-domain or permission evidence.
- Every imported commentary entry still needs author, work title, source URL, edition/date, rights notes, parser notes, and review status.
