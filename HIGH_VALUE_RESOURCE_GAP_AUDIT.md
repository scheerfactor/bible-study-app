## Guardrails

- Do not import modern copyrighted works from Archive.org, Logos lists, scans, or PDFs unless written permission or public-domain evidence is documented.
- Do not duplicate works already in the Library; group volumes/editions under one work card when possible.
- Treat broad sets like Pulpit Commentary, Biblical Illustrator, McClintock and Strong, and Great Preaching of the Faith as multi-batch projects with parser QA.
- Keep doctrinal labels user-friendly: Core Library, Recommended, Historical / Use Carefully, Needs Review, Do Not Include.
- Scripture text and core study recommendations should remain KJV-first.
