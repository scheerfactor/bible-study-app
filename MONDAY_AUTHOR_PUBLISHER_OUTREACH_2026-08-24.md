## Wisdom And Catalog Conversation Email

Subject: `Would you share wisdom for a KJV Bible study app?`

```text
Hello [NAME],

My name is Stephen Scheer. I am building Father's Business Bible Study, a KJV-centered, carefully curated study app for pastors, Sunday School teachers, missionaries, evangelists, Christian schools, and serious Bible students.

The goal is to help a reader move naturally from the KJV text to Webster 1828 definitions, Strong's study, cross-references, reviewed commentary, private notes, sermon or lesson preparation, and presentations. I also want authors and publishers to have a trustworthy way to offer authorized books, audio, and video without losing control of their work.

Before I assume that I know what pastors, readers, authors, or publishers need, would you be willing to share 20 minutes of counsel? I would especially value your thoughts on:

- the study or teaching task that current software makes too slow or difficult
- what would make you comfortable recommending a Bible study app
- how purchased books should open beside relevant KJV passages
- which search, notes, sermon, lesson, audio, video, church, or school features matter most
- what rights protection, reporting, correction, and removal controls a publisher should have

If there is mutual interest afterward, a separate conversation could consider 1-3 named pilot titles. Possible models include purchase through your store with an app unlock, app sales with a publisher-majority revenue share, licensed church or school access, or approved listings and official links. Nothing copyrighted would be copied, hosted, sold, narrated, or indexed without written permission.

Would a short call or private demonstration be useful, and is there someone else who should join a digital-rights conversation?

Thank you,

Stephen Scheer
Father's Business Bible Study
hello@fathersbusinessmasteryresources.com
https://study.fathersbusinessmasteryresources.com/partners
```

## Discovery Questions

Use `PUBLISHER_DISCOVERY_INTERVIEW.md` as the full guide. For a first call, prioritize these six questions:

1. Which single Bible study, sermon, lesson, or classroom workflow should the app do exceptionally well?
2. What would make you comfortable recommending it to pastors, teachers, and students?
3. Which 5-20 titles or series are most useful to serious Bible students?
4. How should an authorized book appear beside a KJV verse or passage?
5. Which purchase, unlock, revenue-share, church, school, or subscription model best serves your readers?
6. What rights, reporting, correction, and takedown controls would you require?

## Follow-Through

- Send a short thank-you after each conversation.
- Return a private summary to the respondent for correction.
- Keep product advice separate from permission and licensing decisions.
- Start any commercial relationship with 1-3 pilot titles, then use a written multi-title catalog framework.
- Record title, edition, territory, formats, search, quotations, verse links, notes, offline use, audio, video, presentation use, price, royalties, reporting, updates, and withdrawal separately.
